## Confirmed from the recording

- **Called "Brother Jim."** A member thanks him afterward: *"Thank you, brother Jim. Excellent
  lesson."* ("James" → "Jim" is the natural reading; Adam gives the full name as James Edgar Green.)
- **Church of Christ gospel preacher.** Marks all present: a cappella congregational singing, the
  invitation, baptism for the remission of sins, KJV preaching, "the lesson" for the sermon, "brother"
  as address.
- **Preached from the King James Version**, from memory — he quotes long passages and occasionally
  self-corrects a chapter-and-verse reference aloud.
- **Normally an outline preacher.** He frames this story-sermon as a departure: *"It is somewhat
  different from the manner in which I usually present lessons."*
- **Worked from a tract by brother David Roper**, whom he credits for the idea.
- **Contemporary: "brother Dave" was preaching in Mount Pleasant** (closing announcements). Whether
  this is the same David Roper, and where this Mount Pleasant is, is unknown.
- **No date is on the recording itself.** (An earlier circa-1990–1991 guess, read off the in-story
  radio "news," is superseded — see *Confirmed by Adam* below. That "news" is scene-setting inside the
  John Jones story, not a datable headline.)
- **Audience cue:** *"you live in the garden spot of it"* (speaking of beautiful local autumns) — he
  is addressing a congregation in a place known for fall color. A clue to region, not a determination.
