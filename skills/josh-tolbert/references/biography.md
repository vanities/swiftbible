# Josh Tolbert — biography

**Rule for this file: only what the recordings actually evidence.** Everything else is a blank for
Adam to fill. Do not invent dates, roles, family, or locations — the same discipline as
[the Brother Jim archive](../../james-edgar-green/references/biography.md).

## Known from the recordings

- Teaches and occasionally preaches at the **Jackson Heights Church of Christ**
  ([thebibleway.org](https://www.thebibleway.org)), the congregation behind the YouTube channel
  the recordings come from.
- **Baptized at age 10.** He tells the story directly: he asked permission twice and was told no,
  *because he was asking permission*; the day it happened he said he was not leaving until they
  baptized him. He describes himself as terrified and crying, and names **fear** as his motive at
  the time — and doesn't think that was necessarily wrong.
- **Lifelong Church of Christ.** *"I have never gone a period of my life not coming to church…
  I have always attended a church of Christ."*
- **Played an instrument** before his singing years — *"Joyful, joyful, we adore thee always gets
  me, too, because it's built off of Ode to Joy. And I always have a little stutter with it because
  I played that in instruments and all of that."*
  (`20210512-…-does-the-origin-of-a-hymn-matter-…-1C72Yy135MA.txt:34–37`)
- **Has a secular job in which he teaches or facilitates groups.** *"When I teach groups at work
  and I'm trying to help individuals to develop their understanding of themselves, I like to ask
  them questions and write it on the board and turn around and say, now what is wrong with this?"*
  (`20250331-…-1QkhEtf0QU4.txt:125–128`); *"I talk to people at work every day that say they want
  to please God, but how they describe it I've never seen in Scripture."*
  (`20211203-…-becoming-children-of-light-uVeYKpFz4GE.txt:181–182`). This is the strongest
  available evidence that he is **not** a full-time preacher — but he never names the job.
- **Married, with a brother; a Christian family on both sides.** *"My parents are Christians, my
  grandparents were Christians, many of my family members are Christians, my wife is a Christian,
  my brother is a Christian, everybody in my life is Christians, all of my close friends are
  Christians."* (`…1QkhEtf0QU4.txt:112–114`). His mother is quoted directly: *"I was taught to be a
  Christian, I am a Christian. That was good enough for her. And I'm grateful because she's still
  a Christian."* (`…1QkhEtf0QU4.txt:451–455`). **He does not name his wife.**
- **Describes his own cast of mind** as *"a fairly critical mind … incredibly skeptical and at
  times borderline rude with my questioning"* (`…1QkhEtf0QU4.txt:123–125`) and as
  *"a knowledge-oriented person"* (`…becoming-children-of-light-uVeYKpFz4GE.txt:176`).
- **Introduced from the pulpit as "Brother Josh Tolbert"** — *"Tonight, Brother Josh Tolbert is
  going to be bringing our lesson to us."*
  (`20251016-…-avoiding-vengeance-…-0TU7R3vJifo.txt:10`). No office is ever named.
- **Teaches whole Old Testament books in sequence**, a book at a time over months — the clearest
  single fact about him. Transcribed series, with the number of transcripts:
  *Jesus in the Minor Prophets* (7, Jul–Oct 2020), **Leviticus** — "God's Definition of Holy",
  Pt. 1–12 minus Pt. 4 (11, Jul–Nov 2021), **1 Samuel** (2, 2022), **2 Samuel** (4, 2023),
  **Ezra** (5, Apr–Jun 2024), **Nehemiah** (7, Jul–Sep 2024), **Deuteronomy** (8, Jan–Mar 2025),
  **Isaiah** (14, Apr–Aug 2026, ongoing).
- **Treats the congregation's teaching as one shared project** — back-references his own earlier
  series ("we dealt with that in Ezra"), flags where a series will land ("we will ultimately get to
  Malachi in this quarter"), and points at colleagues' concurrent classes ("Clay's doing the class
  on Jonah").
- **Taught on hymnody** twice in Sunday-AM class (Apr–May 2021), including whether a hymn's origin
  or ordinary secular use should affect its use in worship.
- **Substituted for Matt Bassford** in the Sunday-AM class and co-taught at least one Wednesday
  class with him ("The Matt and Clay Show," Nov 2020, posted Jan 2021).
- **Spent roughly a year on the deconstruction question** before teaching *Why I Am Still a
  Christian* (Mar 2025), prompted by friends he grew up with leaving the faith and by online
  apologetics against scripture.
- **Colleagues named in the recordings:** Matt Bassford, Clay Gentry, Charlie Norman, Ben,
  Dick Sullivan, Doug Arney, Landon Love.
- **Recorded range:** the manifest attributes **107 recordings, ~86.5 hours** to him, from
  **2018-11-29 to 2026-08-23**. Of those, **87 have transcripts**, of which **73 are substantive
  lessons** (~574,000 words) and the rest are aborted livestream fragments. The transcribed
  material runs **2019-10-03 to 2026-08-23**. He has taught there continuously across that span.

## Relationship to the other speaker archives

holds ~92 hours of Matt's preaching that the blog-post archive has no audio counterpart for.
