# Matt Bassford — Theological positions

Twelve positions reconstructed from his sermons, Bulletin Articles, and arguments across the archive. These are *Matt's* readings — cite faithfully but recognize they are his positions, not the only Christian readings.

## 1. Tradition: non-institutional churches of Christ

The conservative, **anti-cooperation** branch (vs. mainstream "institutional" CoC). He grew up in non-institutional CoC in the 1980s/90s and explicitly identifies with that tradition.

- `2017-07-12-trump-the-media-and-negativity.md`
- `2017-11-14-a-new-to-me-argument-for-church-cooperation.md`
- `2017-11-20-the-why-of-obedience.md`
- `2018-03-09-tradition-and-the-churches-of-christ.md`

## 2. A cappella worship is right; instrumental worship is wrong

Argument from silence + early-church witness (Augustine, Clement). Calls a-cappella congregational singing a *"luminous, shimmering experience."*

- `2016-08-02-joy-and-a-cappella-worship.md`
- `2017-08-14-why-song-worship-matters.md`
- `2018-04-05-the-future-of-song-worship.md`

## 3. Baptism is immersion for the remission of sins

Not merely an outward sign. Explicitly rejects original sin, infant baptism, "baptized because already saved," and "baptized to join a church." Reads Acts 2:38 / 22:16 plainly.

- `2018-03-05-why-should-i-be-baptized.md`
- `2018-09-21-baptized-for-the-dead.md`
- `2018-01-23-the-red-sea-and-baptism.md`

## 4. Lord's Supper: weekly, memorial-symbolic

Every first day of the week. Unleavened bread and fruit of the vine. Anti-transubstantiation. Argues from Acts 20:7 + Troas-Pentecost timing pattern.

- `2017-08-28-revisiting-the-lords-supper.md`
- `2022-08-04-in-the-breaking-of-the-bread.md`
- `2015-08-20-deconstructing-one-bread-one-body.md`

## 5. Women's roles: complementarian (with nuance)

Silent in worship in the sense of not being the speaker / exercising teaching authority over men. Allowed to sing. May speak in Bible classes if doing so submissively. Built on 1 Cor 14:34-35 read with Eph 5:19 and 1 Tim 2:11-12.

- `2021-02-04-women-participating-in-bible-classes.md`

## 6. Anti-premillennial

Kingdom = present reign of Christ over hearts and the church. The church and the kingdom are linked but not identical. Christians still pray *"thy kingdom come"* because the kingdom keeps expanding as more submit.

- `2014-08-05-your-kingdom-come.md`
- `2020-03-09-your-kingdom-come.md`
- `2014-07-10-the-kingdom-of-christ.md`

**Pushback flag:** Any draft that reads "thy kingdom come" purely eschatologically (= second coming) Matt would partially affirm but soften. He'd want at least some weight on "people surrendering to God *now*."

## 7. Hell: literal, eternal, conscious separation from God

Unapologetically defends as just. Anti-universalism. Anti-annihilationism implied. *"He sends them to hell because it's the only option left."*

- `2021-11-11-straight-talk-about-hell.md`
- `2016-03-14-heaven-hell-and-judgment.md`

**Pushback flag:** Any softening of hell, any drift toward universalism, any "everyone ends up okay" framing — Matt resists.

## 8. Anti-Calvinist

Romans 9 is about national Israel vs. spiritual Israel, not individual predestination. Pharaoh hardened his own heart first; God then confirmed it. Free will + responsibility.

- `2021-11-12-calvinism-in-romans-9.md`

## 9. Salvation by grace; obedience as response, not basis of merit

Increasingly emphatic in his last 2 years. Explicitly says only Christ's grace (not his own goodness) gives him hope facing death. Cites Rahab as model: justification by works inseparable from faith. Pushed back against brethren who heard him drifting toward antinomianism.

> *"Grace and obedience are two halves of the same whole."*

- `2022-09-14-salvation-by-grace.md`
- `2022-09-13-the-bible.md` (final sermon)
- `2021-08-18-the-rich-young-ruler-and-grace.md`

## 10. Hymnody: hymns speak FOR the congregation, not TO it

Leader-participant model, not performer-audience. Singability matters (meter, range, intervals). Biblical content over musical style. Rejects "old vs. new" tribalism in worship music.

- `2017-08-14-why-song-worship-matters.md`
- `2018-04-05-the-future-of-song-worship.md`
- `2021-03-03-in-praise-of-hymns-with-verses.md`
- `2019-06-07-singing-hymns-as-written.md`
- `2022-11-29-how-to-write-hymns-the-super-brief-version.md`

## 11. Bible translations: prefers formal-equivalent for study

Primary Bible was a Crossway Large-Print Thinline Reference (ESV). Recommends NASB, NKJV, ESV, CSB. Uses freer translations (NIV, NLT, CSB) for daily reading. Rejects The Message paraphrase. Wrote 164 Bible Reviews — translation pickiness was a hobby, not a hill to die on.

- `2017-11-16-choosing-a-reading-bible-type-and-translation.md`
- `2017-01-13-crossway-single-column-journaling-bible-preaching-teaching-and-study.md`
- `2017-11-29-choosing-a-reading-bible-recommendations.md`

## 12. Other distinctives

- **Mental health: pro-medication for Christians.** Defends antidepressants against brethren who'd think them un-spiritual. He took Trazodone + Lexapro starting July 2021. (`2022-01-14-antidepressants-six-months-in.md`)
- **Apologetics: presuppositional-leaning.** Problem of evil best answered existentially, not philosophically. *"We make decisions with the Biblical heart… not the Western mind."* (`2021-07-07-the-problem-of-other-peoples-suffering.md`)
- **Self-righteousness is the great religious-people sin.** Apply rebuking Scripture to yourself first. (`2022-09-13-the-bible.md`)
- **Anti-Catholic on tradition** but appreciative of patristic witness when it converges with NT-only practice. (`2016-08-02-joy-and-a-cappella-worship.md`)

## How to use this when drafting

If a devotional brushes a CoC distinctive (worship, baptism, communion, kingdom theology, hell), check Matt's anchor post. He may agree, partially agree, or push back. The pushback flags above are the high-frequency tripwires.

If a devotional is on grace, suffering, or eternal life — Matt's late writing is rich and theologically careful (post-2021 especially). Don't lift his quotes wholesale; absorb the structure of his argument.
