# Matt Bassford — Theological positions

Twelve positions reconstructed from his sermons, Bulletin Articles, and arguments across the archive. These are *Matt's* readings — cite faithfully but recognize they are his positions, not the only Christian readings.

## 1. Tradition: non-institutional churches of Christ

The conservative, **anti-cooperation** branch (vs. mainstream "institutional" CoC). He grew up in non-institutional CoC in the 1980s/90s and explicitly identifies with that tradition.

- `2017-07-12-trump-the-media-and-negativity.md`
- `2017-11-14-a-new-to-me-argument-for-church-cooperation.md`
- `2017-11-20-the-why-of-obedience.md`
- `2018-03-09-tradition-and-the-churches-of-christ.md`

## 2. A cappella worship is right; instrumental worship is wrong

Argument from silence + early-church witness (Augustine, Clement). Calls a-cappella congregational singing a *"luminous, shimmering experience."*

- `2016-08-02-joy-and-a-cappella-worship.md`
- `2017-08-14-why-song-worship-matters.md`
- `2018-04-05-the-future-of-song-worship.md`

## 3. Baptism is immersion for the remission of sins

Not merely an outward sign. Explicitly rejects original sin, infant baptism, "baptized because already saved," and "baptized to join a church." Reads Acts 2:38 / 22:16 plainly.

- `2018-03-05-why-should-i-be-baptized.md`
- `2018-09-21-baptized-for-the-dead.md`
- `2018-01-23-the-red-sea-and-baptism.md`

## 4. Lord's Supper: weekly, memorial-symbolic

Every first day of the week. Unleavened bread and fruit of the vine. Anti-transubstantiation. Argues from Acts 20:7 + Troas-Pentecost timing pattern.

- `2017-08-28-revisiting-the-lords-supper.md`
- `2022-08-04-in-the-breaking-of-the-bread.md`
- `2015-08-20-deconstructing-one-bread-one-body.md`

## 5. Women's roles: complementarian (with nuance)

Silent in worship in the sense of not being the speaker / exercising teaching authority over men. Allowed to sing. May speak in Bible classes if doing so submissively. Built on 1 Cor 14:34-35 read with Eph 5:19 and 1 Tim 2:11-12.

- `2021-02-04-women-participating-in-bible-classes.md`

## 6. Anti-premillennial

Kingdom = present reign of Christ over hearts and the church. The church and the kingdom are linked but not identical. Christians still pray *"thy kingdom come"* because the kingdom keeps expanding as more submit.

- `2014-08-05-your-kingdom-come.md`
- `2020-03-09-your-kingdom-come.md`
- `2014-07-10-the-kingdom-of-christ.md`

**Pushback flag:** Any draft that reads "thy kingdom come" purely eschatologically (= second coming) Matt would partially affirm but soften. He'd want at least some weight on "people surrendering to God *now*."

## 7. Hell: literal, eternal, conscious separation from God

Unapologetically defends as just. Anti-universalism. Anti-annihilationism implied. *"He sends them to hell because it's the only option left."*

- `2021-11-11-straight-talk-about-hell.md`
- `2016-03-14-heaven-hell-and-judgment.md`

**Pushback flag:** Any softening of hell, any drift toward universalism, any "everyone ends up okay" framing — Matt resists.

## 8. Anti-Calvinist

Romans 9 is about national Israel vs. spiritual Israel, not individual predestination. Pharaoh hardened his own heart first; God then confirmed it. Free will + responsibility.

- `2021-11-12-calvinism-in-romans-9.md`

## 9. Salvation by grace; obedience as response, not basis of merit

Increasingly emphatic in his last 2 years. Explicitly says only Christ's grace (not his own goodness) gives him hope facing death. Cites Rahab as model: justification by works inseparable from faith. Pushed back against brethren who heard him drifting toward antinomianism.

> *"Grace and obedience are two halves of the same whole."*

- `2022-09-14-salvation-by-grace.md`
- `2022-09-13-the-bible.md` (final sermon)
- `2021-08-18-the-rich-young-ruler-and-grace.md`

## 10. Hymnody: hymns speak FOR the congregation, not TO it

Leader-participant model, not performer-audience. Singability matters (meter, range, intervals). Biblical content over musical style. Rejects "old vs. new" tribalism in worship music.

- `2017-08-14-why-song-worship-matters.md`
- `2018-04-05-the-future-of-song-worship.md`
- `2021-03-03-in-praise-of-hymns-with-verses.md`
- `2019-06-07-singing-hymns-as-written.md`
- `2022-11-29-how-to-write-hymns-the-super-brief-version.md`

## 11. Bible translations: prefers formal-equivalent for study

Primary Bible was a Crossway Large-Print Thinline Reference (ESV). Recommends NASB, NKJV, ESV, CSB. Uses freer translations (NIV, NLT, CSB) for daily reading. Rejects The Message paraphrase. Wrote 164 Bible Reviews — translation pickiness was a hobby, not a hill to die on.

- `2017-11-16-choosing-a-reading-bible-type-and-translation.md`
- `2017-01-13-crossway-single-column-journaling-bible-preaching-teaching-and-study.md`
- `2017-11-29-choosing-a-reading-bible-recommendations.md`

## 12. Other distinctives

- **Mental health: pro-medication for Christians.** Defends antidepressants against brethren who'd think them un-spiritual. He took Trazodone + Lexapro starting July 2021. (`2022-01-14-antidepressants-six-months-in.md`)
- **Apologetics: presuppositional-leaning.** Problem of evil best answered existentially, not philosophically. *"We make such decisions with the Biblical heart, the Eastern mind-and-heart, not the Western mind."* (`2021-07-07-the-problem-of-other-peoples-suffering.md`)
- **Self-righteousness is the great religious-people sin.** Apply rebuking Scripture to yourself first. (`2022-09-13-the-bible.md`)
- **Anti-Catholic on tradition** but appreciative of patristic witness when it converges with NT-only practice. (`2016-08-02-joy-and-a-cappella-worship.md`)

## Additional anchor posts by position

These are 2–3 additional anchor posts beyond what's already cited above for each major doctrinal position. Especially valuable: posts where Matt addresses the position **against** a counter-position. Those reveal his actual argumentative shape, which is more useful than topical summaries.

### On baptism (against original sin / infant baptism / sinner's prayer / "to join a church")

- `2018-03-05-why-should-i-be-baptized.md` is the central sermon, but watch for the **rebuttals to specific counter-positions** within it. He rebuts: original sin (by Ezekiel 18:20), Jesus-imitation framing (by noting Jesus was sinless), "outward sign of inward grace" (by Acts 2:38 grammatical analysis), and "to join a church" (by Acts 2:46-47 + Romans 6:3).
- `2019-02-13-the-hope-that-is-in-us.md` — baptism understood **through the resurrection**. *"If the resurrection is the central event of Christianity, it ought also to be the central event of becoming a Christian. What does the sinner's prayer have to do with the resurrection? Nothing! What does baptism have to do with the resurrection? Everything!"*

### On hell (against universalism, annihilationism, and "hell-as-not-so-bad")

- `2021-11-11-straight-talk-about-hell.md` is the central sermon. Note the move from Mark 9:42-48 (hell as worse than mutilation) to Revelation 21:6-8 (hell is for sinners) to John 14:5-6 (only Jesus saves).
- The argument structure is exemplary: he doesn't merely assert hell exists; he answers the *"how can a loving God…?"* objection by enumerating God's prior efforts (creation-witness, conscience, the Son, the kindness of providence, suffering as warning) and concluding *"He sends them to hell because it's the only option left."*
- `2018-12-03-heaven-the-eternal-party.md` — companion piece. *"It makes for an amusing rebuttal to the people who claim to be followers of a sweetness-and-light Jesus while rejecting the teachings of His mean ole followers. Wherever they found their Jesus, they didn't find Him in the Scriptures."*

### On grace + obedience (against antinomianism AND against works-based merit)

- `2022-09-14-salvation-by-grace.md` is the explicit defense against brethren who feared he was drifting toward antinomianism. Key move: Romans 6 against "do-nothing faith" — grace doesn't release us from obligation; it puts us under it. Then James 2 + Rahab as the controlling proof that justification by works is inseparable from genuine faith.
- `2021-08-18-the-rich-young-ruler-and-grace.md` — the matched argument from the other side. The ruler's problem isn't merely greed; it's *"What good must I do to inherit eternal life?"* — wanting to be saved by works. Paired with the parable of the vineyard workers as Matthew's commentary.
- `2022-09-13-the-bible.md` — final-sermon synthesis. *"Grace and obedience are two halves of the same whole."*
- `2022-02-14-too-rotten-for-god.md` — the Saul-of-Tarsus apologetic for the "too rotten" Christian.

### On a cappella worship (against instrumental, but *for* old AND new hymns)

- `2016-08-02-joy-and-a-cappella-worship.md` — the patristic-witness argument, the *"luminous, shimmering experience"* line.
- `2018-04-05-the-future-of-song-worship.md` — the structural argument against worship-wars old-vs-new tribalism. *"Old is not good; new is not good. Good is good."* Defends both better hymns AND better contemporary songs.
- `2017-08-14-why-song-worship-matters.md` — the deeper rationale beyond practice-rule.

### On the local congregation (against abstract "the Church")

- The clearest single post is `2017-04-10-why-come-to-church-more.md` — sermon distinguishing attending vs. encouraging vs. being-encouraged vs. being-influenced.
- `2022-09-13-the-bible.md` — the local-congregation theology in distilled form (Jackson Heights as the unit, the trio of kindness/unity/love-of-Bible).
- `2017-12-12-comfort-and-celebration.md` — referenced in `essential-reads.md`.
- `2015-02-02-why-i-want-to-go-to-heaven.md` — heaven is filled with the named people of the local congregation, not anonymous saints.

### On kingdom theology (against premillennialism, against once-and-only-once, against "the kingdom is just the church")

- `2014-08-05-your-kingdom-come.md` is the early articulation.
- `2020-03-09-your-kingdom-come.md` is the later, more developed version: *"the coming of God's kingdom as something that happens not once, but multiply. It occurs whenever God asserts His dominion and His sovereignty is revealed."*
- `2014-07-10-the-kingdom-of-christ.md` — original CoC over-correction critique.

**Pushback flag (sharpened):** A draft that reads "thy kingdom come" as a *future-only* prayer Matt would partially affirm but soften with present-reign emphasis. A draft that flattens "kingdom = church" entirely Matt would *also* push back on — that was the over-correction his 2020 post explicitly rebukes.

### On suffering (against both the philosophical-problem-of-evil AND the prosperity gospel)

- `2021-07-07-the-problem-of-other-peoples-suffering.md` — the **existential** answer to suffering. Refuses to treat the problem of evil as a Western-rationalist debate. Suffering is answered *experientially*, not philosophically.
- `2022-02-10-a-man-is-judged-by-his-strength.md` — the personal-testimony version. Strength as spiritual blindspot; weakness as the teacher comfort was hiding.
- `2022-08-16-uselessness.md` — the suffering-as-witness extension. James 5:10 read with structural attention to verb tenses.
- `2022-05-25-the-fellowship-of-suffering.md` — sermon framing suffering as participation in Christ.

### On free will / responsibility (against Calvinism, against works-merit)

- `2021-11-12-calvinism-in-romans-9.md` is the explicit anti-Calvinist post.
- `2022-05-12-glorifying-god-the-hard-way.md` — humans WILL glorify God; the only choice is the easy way (willing obedience now) or the hard way (eternal ruin commemorating God's power). Free will is real, but its outcomes resolve into one of two divine outcomes.
- `2018-01-16-pharaohs-hardened-heart.md` — Pharaoh hardened his own heart first; God then confirmed it.

### On mental health (against "Christians shouldn't need meds")

- `2022-01-14-antidepressants-six-months-in.md` — the explicit defense.
- `2022-01-20-creativity-and-depression.md` — companion piece.
- The argument structure: God in the Bible expects suffering, and antidepressants are no different from eyeglasses, antibiotics, or knee replacements. The novelty isn't in the dependence — it's in the technology.

## How to use this when drafting

If a devotional brushes a CoC distinctive (worship, baptism, communion, kingdom theology, hell), check Matt's anchor post. He may agree, partially agree, or push back. The pushback flags above are the high-frequency tripwires.

If a devotional is on grace, suffering, or eternal life — Matt's late writing is rich and theologically careful (post-2021 especially). Don't lift his quotes wholesale; absorb the structure of his argument.

**Specifically for AI prompting:** Matt's theological strength is that his arguments have *shape* — they refuse a position by enumerating its problems, then take a counter-position. If an AI is using this skill to imitate his theology, the answer-by-shape (refuse → enumerate → take) is the structural move that will most reliably register as "Matt-flavored."
