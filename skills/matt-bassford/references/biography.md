## Early life and education

- **Matthew William Bassford**, born **September 22, 1978** in **Somers Point, New Jersey**, to Ronald and Mary Lou (Geisler) Bassford. **[obit]** Consistent with the blog: he was 18 at the first Hymninar in Columbia, MO, in 1997 (`2015-11-10-providence-in-retrospect.md`).
- Family moved to **Columbia, Missouri**, where he grew up; graduated Hickman High School, 1997. **[obit]**
- Grew up in non-institutional churches of Christ in the 1980s/90s.
- **University of Missouri** — bachelor's degrees in **journalism** and **religious studies**. **[obit]** The blog confirms the religious-studies coursework without naming the school (`2021-07-07-the-problem-of-other-peoples-suffering.md`).
- **University of Texas School of Law**, law degree **2003** **[obit]**; he calls it a *"Top 15 law school"* (`2015-01-15-my-experience-with-single-parenthood.md`, `2016-09-08-priorities.md`, `2017-12-06-the-argument-jesus-lost.md`). There he met Lauren and roomed with David Raif (`2015-11-10-providence-in-retrospect.md`). He passed the Texas bar (`2018-11-19-faithfulness-not-perfection.md`) but chose preaching over practice. **[obit]**
- Brother **Andrew** — Matt would "likely have followed my brother to law school at the University of Virginia" had the Hymninar not redirected him (`2015-11-10-providence-in-retrospect.md`). Sister **Susan**. **[obit]**

## Family

- **Wife: Lauren** (née Richardson). Married **Jan 2005** (`2016-01-08-11-years-married.md`). Met at UT Law.
- **Daughter Mary Catherine "Macy"** **[obit]** — stillborn at full term **Sept 19, 2008**; cause never determined (`2015-09-29-bill-nye-whiffs-on-abortion-part-1.md`, `2015-10-20-talking-about-macy-part-1.md`).
- **Daughter Zoë** — born ~2010 (age 4 in Sept 2014). Red-headed, stubborn (`2014-09-04-the-hermeneutic-of-zoë.md`, `2022-09-20-sons.md`).
- **Son Mark / "Marky"** — born ~2011 ("he remains an 11-year-old boy" in Sept 2022). *"He is so much like me that at times I feel like an outside observer looking at myself."* (`2022-09-20-sons.md`).
- **Mother, Mary Lou** — died **2011** (*"It belonged to my mother and passed to me after her death in 2011"* — `2014-03-21-what-to-do-with-an-scr-02.md`; corroborated by `2016-01-08-11-years-married.md`: *"the year my mother died; the year our daughter died"*). She had both **ALS and frontotemporal dementia**, which are *"genetically linked"* in his family (`2021-07-08-in-christ-i-will-not-lose.md`). This genetic link is what shaped his late voice — he had context for what was coming.
- **Father, Ronald** — also predeceased him. **[obit]**

## Hobbies (pre-illness)

- Hunting and hiking (mourned losing both — `2022-01-11-to-live-is-christ-to-die-is-gain.md`)
- Hymn writing — collaborator with **Sumphonia** (Charli Couchman, Craig Roberts, Richard Morrison, Glenda Schales). Collaborated with Charli on *"Exalted"* (1999) and worked on *"Savior and Friend"*, *"Servant's Song"*, and *"The Rock of My Heart"* with Craig, Richard Morrison, and Glenda Schales respectively, in 2000 (`2015-11-10-providence-in-retrospect.md`); *"I'll Never Weep Alone"* appeared on Sing to the Lord's album *The Bible Stands* (`2016-08-04-ill-never-weep-alone.md`).
- Published **A Psalter** (2021) — all 150 psalms paraphrased into English rhyme and meter and set to familiar tunes; also workbooks and study books. **[obit]** *Not attested anywhere in the blog* — the archive goes quiet on psalters after 2019. Do not conflate it with ***Timeless***, the separate, collaborative modern-day psalter whose editorial board he sat on (`2017-12-14-the-work-of-an-evangelist-circa-2017.md`, `2019-02-07-we-should-sing-more-psalms.md`, `2019-02-19-out-of-the-depths.md`) and which he explicitly said *"certainly isn't my brainchild."*
- Pre-illness exercise: biking, kickboxing, pilates (`2022-04-05-passing-for-normal.md`).

## ALS timeline

This is the gravitational center of his late voice. Everything in the second half of the archive is shaped by this arc.

- **Summer 2021** — mental-health crisis. Began Trazodone + Lexapro July 2021 ("the reality of my terminal diagnosis was dawning on me" — `2022-01-14-antidepressants-six-months-in.md`).
- **July 8, 2021** — first explicit ALS-as-word mention (`2021-07-08-in-christ-i-will-not-lose.md`). References his mother had ALS+FTD.
- **August 13, 2021** — references "my struggles with ALS" and being "a spectacle" (`2021-08-13-a-spectacle.md`).
- **Diagnosis date** — **July 2021**, stated directly a year later: *"Since I was diagnosed with ALS last July…"* (`2022-07-06-thoughts-and-prayers.md`, dated July 6, 2022). Corroborated by the first blog mention on July 8, 2021 and by *"I recently was diagnosed with ALS, Lou Gehrig's disease"* (`2021-08-25-a-message-to-x.md`).
- **Voice shift** — writing turns more meditative, mortality-soaked, grace-emphatic. Bulletin Articles and Meditations dominate over Sermons. Repeated themes: usefulness, grief over not finishing raising kids, suffering as witness.
- **April 2022** — capable only of stretching and walking; needed help with chairs (`2022-04-05-passing-for-normal.md`).
- **August 2022** — hands "crippled and nearly useless," writes by dictation (`2022-08-16-uselessness.md`).
- **Aug 28, 2022** — final sermon at Jackson Heights, published two weeks later as `2022-09-13-the-bible.md`. (`2022-08-24-leaving-the-pulpit.md`, a Wednesday, says *"Next Sunday morning, I will preach my last sermon ever for the Jackson Heights church"*; the Aug 21 "Unity" Bible class in the audio corpus says *"next Sunday will be their last Sunday with us."* Beware: post dates are **publication** dates, not delivery dates.) Closes a trio on Jackson Heights's core attributes (kindness, unity, love of the Bible) — the first two are in the sermon corpus as the two Aug 2022 "Unity" classes.
- **Late Sept 2022** — moves to Texas, joins Kleinwood as a member.
- **Jan 23, 2023** — final personal post (`2023-01-23-white-noise.md`): "So apparently I have pneumonia and a partially collapsed lung."
- **Death — Wednesday, October 25, 2023**, at a hospice facility, age 45. **[obit]** Consistent with the two corpus signals: Lauren's tribute of May 15, 2024 says *"It's been just over 6 months since Matt went to meet Jesus"* (`2024-05-15-a-new-life.md`), and the Jackson Heights memorial service was held Nov 4, 2023. Visitation was Oct 27 at Klein Funeral Home Champions; a first memorial service was held at the Kleinwood church building Oct 28; the Jackson Heights service followed Nov 4. His ashes were scattered privately. **[obit]**
- **May 15, 2024** — Lauren posts the final tribute (`2024-05-15-a-new-life.md`), announcing the blog will continue as a landing page for Matt's writing and unpublished material she intends to release.

## How to use this biography when drafting

- If a devotional touches **suffering, mortality, illness, weakness, dependency** — Matt's late posts (2022–2023) are your richest material. He lived these.
- If a devotional touches **family, marriage, children** — `2015-10-20-talking-about-macy-part-1.md` (grief), `2016-01-08-11-years-married.md` (marriage), `2022-09-20-sons.md` (raising kids), `2022-09-13-the-bible.md` (his final words to his congregation).
- If a devotional touches **vocation, usefulness, service** — `2022-08-16-uselessness.md` is the centerpiece.
- If a devotional touches **the local church** — virtually any sermon, but especially `2022-09-13-the-bible.md` and `2017-12-12-comfort-and-celebration.md`.
- If a devotional touches **hymns, worship, song** — the Hymn Theory archive (~100 posts) is the deepest treatment.
