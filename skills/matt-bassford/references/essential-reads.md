# Matt Bassford — Essential reads

20 most representative posts, categorized by use case. If you only had time to read 20 of his 1639 posts to understand him, these are the ones.

## Late voice / post-ALS (the gravitational center)

These are the indispensable six. Everything in his late writing is in here.

1. **`2022-01-11-to-live-is-christ-to-die-is-gain.md`** — Phil 1:21 from a terminal-diagnosis position. The single most-cited Matt post for any devotional touching mortality.
2. **`2022-09-13-the-bible.md`** — His final sermon at Jackson Heights. Trio closer (kindness, unity, love of the Bible). Grace + obedience as "two halves of the same whole." Self-righteousness as religious-people sin. Closes with "When We All Get to Heaven."
3. **`2022-08-16-uselessness.md`** — Hands "crippled and nearly useless," writes by dictation. John Milton's blindness sonnet. Suffering and patience as final witness. Closes: *"May I have the courage to take it."*
4. **`2022-12-07-disgusting.md`** — Grace despite disgust. ALS humiliation as theological access point. *"We can never be so disgusting that God will cease to love us."*
5. **`2023-01-23-white-noise.md`** — Last personal post. Pneumonia + collapsed lung from ALS. Sin as chronic illness. The fiercest closing in the archive.
6. **`2022-03-17-its-not-a-wonderful-world.md`** — Anti-secular-optimism. Lviv refugee + COVID + ALS as proof that the world is unfixable. *"This world isn't wonderful, and it never will be."*

## Heaven & eternity

7. **`2015-02-02-why-i-want-to-go-to-heaven.md`** — Heaven as relational. *"Hell isn't other people. Heaven is."* Critical for any heaven-themed devotional.
8. **`2018-12-03-heaven-the-eternal-party.md`** — Heaven as banquet/feast. Wedding-feast imagery. Lazarus reclining at Abraham's bosom.
9. **`2018-04-23-after-are-you-heaven-bound.md`** — Practical-application sermon on preparing for the day of judgment.

## Voice formation / early (the wry settled register)

10. **`2014-02-18-introducing-my-blog.md`** — His first post. The voice is already largely there — wry, methodical, slightly self-deprecating.
11. **`2014-08-05-your-kingdom-come.md`** — Anti-premillennialism. His kingdom theology in under 300 words.
12. **`2016-09-28-fixing-our-hope.md`** — 1 Peter 1:12 (the verse a Facebook friend posted, which sets the post off). The law-school grading metaphor. Closes: *"There is no other sensible way to live our lives than to go all-in on Jesus."*

## Theological positions (where his arguments show)

13. **`2018-03-05-why-should-i-be-baptized.md`** — Baptism for remission of sins. The cleanest articulation of his CoC distinctive on baptism.
14. **`2016-08-02-joy-and-a-cappella-worship.md`** — A cappella worship. Augustine, Clement, the "luminous, shimmering experience" line.
15. **`2021-11-11-straight-talk-about-hell.md`** — Hell, no universalism, no annihilationism. Look-here-at-Mark-9:42-48 cadence.
16. **`2022-09-14-salvation-by-grace.md`** — Salvation by grace, obedience as response. Clarifies he's not antinomian.

## Hymns (if a devotional touches singing/worship)

17. **`2018-04-05-the-future-of-song-worship.md`** — Hymns speak FOR the congregation. The broadest theological case for his hymnody.
18. **`2018-01-17-hiding-in-hymns.md`** — Hymns as costly obedience theme.

## Personal / family (essential biographical context)

19. **`2015-10-20-talking-about-macy-part-1.md`** — Macy stillbirth (Sept 19, 2008). Crucial for any grief-themed work and for understanding how he writes about loss.
20. **`2024-05-15-a-new-life.md`** — Wife Lauren's tribute, posted ~6 months after his death. Not Matt's voice but essential biographical/contextual close.

## How to use this list

- **Drafting any devotional?** Read the six post-ALS posts (1-6 above). His late voice is where his theology and rhetoric most fully cohere.
- **Drafting on a specific theme?** Cross-reference [themes.md](themes.md) for that theme's anchor posts, then add any relevant ones from this list.
- **Want to understand his theology in 90 minutes?** Read 1, 2, 11, 13, 14, 15, 16. That's enough to recognize his positions.
- **Want to understand his voice in 30 minutes?** Read 1, 2, 5, 6. The post-ALS posts have his sharpest voice tells most concentrated.
