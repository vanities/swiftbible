## Who Matt was

Non-institutional Church of Christ preacher. Born Sept 22, 1978 (Somers Point, NJ); raised in Columbia, MO. University of Missouri (journalism + religious studies), then UT Law, class of 2003 — where he met his wife Lauren. Passed the bar, chose preaching instead. Four congregations:

| Years | Congregation | Location |
|---|---|---|
| ~2004 (*date unconfirmed*) | Spring Woods church of Christ | Houston, TX |
| mid-2000s | Dowlen Rd. church of Christ (preacher training) | Beaumont, TX |
| ~2005–2017 | Joliet church of Christ | Joliet, IL |
| 2017–Sept 2022 | Jackson Heights church of Christ | Columbia, TN |

Hymn writer and theorist (collaborator with Sumphonia). Avid hunter and hiker before illness.

**ALS arc.** Diagnosed **July 2021** — *"Since I was diagnosed with ALS last July"* (`2022-07-06-thoughts-and-prayers.md`), the same disease that took his mother, genetically linked in his family. First explicit mention: `2021-07-08-in-christ-i-will-not-lose.md`. Final sermon at Jackson Heights: preached Aug 28, 2022, published as `2022-09-13-the-bible.md`. Final personal post: `2023-01-23-white-noise.md`. Died **Oct 25, 2023**, age 45.

**Family.** Wife Lauren (née Richardson, married Jan 2005). First child Mary Catherine "Macy" stillborn at full term (Sept 19, 2008). Daughter Zoë (b. ~2010). Son Mark/Marky (b. ~2011).

**The ALS arc is essential context.** His pre-2021 writing is wry, careful, Bible-geek precise. His post-diagnosis writing (2021–2023) is the gravitational center of his late voice — meditative, mortality-soaked, grace-emphatic, sometimes startlingly direct.

For full biographical detail, see [references/biography.md](references/biography.md).

## Voice fingerprint (top tells)

When suggesting Matt-flavored phrasing or recognizing his voice, watch for:

1. **Flat single-line confession.** Bald admission as a complete paragraph. *"I hold grudges. It is one of my biggest spiritual failings."* (`2023-01-02-grudges.md`); *"I am. I know it. ALS robs its victims of dignity along with everything else."* (`2022-12-07-disgusting.md`)

2. **Inverted/paradox aphorism.** Pivots a cliche or expectation. *"Hell isn't other people. Heaven is."* (`2015-02-02-why-i-want-to-go-to-heaven.md`); *"This world isn't wonderful, and it never will be. Our Savior is wonderful, and He never will be anything else."* (`2022-03-17-its-not-a-wonderful-world.md`); *"To live is Christ and to die is Christ."* (`2017-01-09-philippians-part-1.md`)

3. **Concrete domestic detail as on-ramp.** Posts open in laundry rooms, hair salons, with Boomer the chihuahua, with Marky on the couch. *"My in-laws own a Chihuahua named Boomer."* (`2022-04-25-loyalty.md`); *"Yesterday, I got my hair cut."* (`2022-04-05-passing-for-normal.md`); *"So apparently I have pneumonia and a partially collapsed lung."* (`2023-01-23-white-noise.md`)

4. **Verdict-style closing imperatives.** Three to five short clauses, command voice, no flourish. *"Fear them. Hate them. Fling them away from you as you would a viper or scorpion."* (`2023-01-23-white-noise.md`); *"Create. Give. Lose. The world will think you're an idiot, but God won't."* (`2022-05-04-create-more-than-you-consume.md`)

5. **Bible-geek precision.** Splits a verse into halves and argues each one; names paper weights and editors. *"it doesn't really reckon with either half of Philippians 1:21. Let's start with the back half."* (`2022-01-11-to-live-is-christ-to-die-is-gain.md`); *"the 38-gsm Tervakowski ThinOpaque in my Cambridge Wide-Margin"* (`2017-01-13-crossway-single-column-journaling-bible-preaching-teaching-and-study.md`)

6. **Self-aware aside in parentheses.** Wry footnote on himself mid-argument. *"(as my poor wife has discovered far too often)"* (`2023-01-02-grudges.md`); *"(though I certainly would have been thankful if they had!)"* (`2022-07-06-thoughts-and-prayers.md`)

7. **Specific congregation/place anchor.** Names Westley Pollard at Dowlen Rd., Clay's rule of thumb, Jackson Heights, Joliet — never abstract "the Church." *"The most successful personal worker I've ever known is Westley Pollard, elder of the Dowlen Rd. church in Beaumont."* (`2022-04-05-passing-for-normal.md`)

8. **Dry sarcasm against secular pieties.** Italicized or capitalized scorn aimed at progressive cliches, NBC News framing, "thoughts and prayers" mockery. *"Of course, NBC painted her as a Symbol Of Hope amid devastation and despair, a promise of Better Days Ahead."* (`2022-03-17-its-not-a-wonderful-world.md`)

For the full voice analysis with more tells and examples, see [references/voice.md](references/voice.md).

## Recurring themes (top 10)

1. **Heaven as relational fulfillment, not real estate** — heaven matters because the people we love and Christ are there
2. **Suffering as clarifying lens** — pain doesn't get explained, it strips illusions
3. **Grace despite disgust** — God loathes sin and rescues anyway; grace is not exhausted at baptism
4. **The local congregation as primary Christian unit** — Joliet, Jackson Heights, Dowlen Rd. — never "the Church" abstract
5. **Hymns speak FOR the congregation, not TO it** — leader-participant model
6. **Self-reliance/strength as spiritual blindspot** — strength shapes worldview; weakness teaches what comfort hid
7. **Usefulness as the metric of a life** — service to people you may never see again
8. **The world is broken and won't be fixed** — hostile to secular optimism / "wonderful world" framing
9. **Costly obedience over comfortable normalcy** — faith requires looking weird
10. **Self-righteousness as the great religious-people sin** — apply rebuke to yourself first

For each theme with 3–5 anchor posts and quotes, see [references/themes.md](references/themes.md).

## Theological pushback flags

When offering Matt-flavored phrasing or measuring a draft against his voice, watch for these — places he would push back:

- **World-improvement framing** — Matt calls it doomed. The world won't be fixed; it will be consumed and remade.
- **Feelings-only piety with no obedience** — for Matt, grace and obedience are *"two halves of the same whole."*
- **Softening hell** — he names it explicitly; refuses universalism and annihilationism.
- **Abstract "Church" without local congregation** — for Matt, Christianity is always Joliet or Jackson Heights, never just "the Church."
- **Closing flourish that's poetic instead of imperative** — his late closings are verdicts, not prayers.
- **Heaven-as-place language without heaven-as-people** — he reads heaven relationally.
- **Premillennial / literal-millennial reading of "thy kingdom come"** — he reads kingdom as Christ's present reign over hearts and the church, not a future earthly reign.
- **Treating mental-health medication as un-spiritual** — he was on antidepressants and defended that for Christians.

For the full theological positions with anchor posts, see [references/theology.md](references/theology.md).

## How to use this archive

### Search what Matt said on a theme

```bash
python3 .claude/skills/matt-bassford/scripts/search.py "second coming" --top 5
python3 .claude/skills/matt-bassford/scripts/search.py "suffering" --label "Sermons" --top 10
python3 .claude/skills/matt-bassford/scripts/search.py "ALS grace" --since 2021 --top 5
```

Run it from the repo root (the script walks up from its own location to find `speakers/matt-bassford/posts/`, so any cwd inside the repo works). Query terms are OR'd; the script ranks by total term frequency and returns title + filename + snippet for each match. Filters: `--label` (Sermons / Meditations / Bulletin Articles / Bible Reviews / Hymns / Hymn Theory / Studies in Character) and date with `--since` / `--until` (YYYY or YYYY-MM-DD).

**`search.py` searches the blog posts only.** `--label Sermons` filters on the *blog* label (302 posts he wrote up as sermons), not on the audio transcripts. To search the sermon transcripts, grep them directly:

```bash
rg -i -C2 "brethren" speakers/matt-bassford/sermons/*.txt
```

The archive is at `speakers/matt-bassford/posts/`. Filenames are `YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md` with YAML frontmatter (title, date, url, labels, id). Use `Read` directly when you have a filename.
