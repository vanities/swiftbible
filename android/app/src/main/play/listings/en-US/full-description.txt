SwiftBible is a quiet, beautiful place to read scripture.

THE TEXTS YOU KNOW
• King James Version (KJV) — the classic, 1611
• American Standard Version (ASV) — literal, scholarly, 1901
• World English Bible (WEB) — modern English, public domain
• Original Hebrew & Greek source texts

AND THE TEXTS YOU MAY NOT
• Deuterocanonical Apocrypha — Tobit, Judith, Wisdom of Solomon, Ecclesiasticus, Baruch, 1 & 2 Maccabees, additions to Esther and Daniel, plus Orthodox-only books
• 1 Enoch — five sections including the Watchers, Parables, and Astronomical Book
• 2 Enoch (Slavonic), Jubilees, Testaments of the Twelve Patriarchs
• Didache and 1 Clement — earliest Christian writings outside the canon

A BEAUTIFUL READING EXPERIENCE
• Light, Dark, and Sepia reading themes
• Choose between Lora, Source Serif, Cormorant Garamond, or Inter
• Adjustable font size (12–30pt)
• Jesus's words shown in red in the gospels
• Chapter and passage summaries — at-a-glance overviews

MAKE IT YOURS
• Highlight verses in six colors
• Write notes on any verse
• Bookmark verses to return to
• Resume from where you left off

DAILY DEVOTIONAL
A fresh verse and reflection every day, with a different theme — faith, hope, love, peace, joy, strength, wisdom, courage.

WORKS COMPLETELY OFFLINE
The entire library is bundled with the app. No account, no sign-in, no internet required.

NO ADS. NO TRACKING.
SwiftBible does not collect data about you. Your highlights, notes, and history stay on your device.

Open source and made with care.
