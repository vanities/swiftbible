My Bible reading this morning was about the ordination of Aaron and his sons in Leviticus 8-10.  In this story, a variation of "as the LORD commanded Moses" appears 11 times, in 8:4, 8:5, 8:9, 8:13, 8:17, 8:21, 8:29, 8:36, 9:7, 9:10, and 9:21.  It is at the end of this sequence that we read 10:1-2:

"Now Nadab and Abihu, the sons of Aaron, each took his censer and put fire in it and offered unauthorized fire before the LORD, which he had not commanded them.  And fire came out from before the LORD and consumed them, and they died before the LORD." (ESV)

In more than one sense, Nadab and Abihu broke the pattern.
