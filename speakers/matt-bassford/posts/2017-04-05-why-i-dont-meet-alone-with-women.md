Last week, a media tempest in a teapot arose over Vice
President Mike Pence’s revelation that he will not meet alone with a woman, nor
attend an event where alcohol is served unless his wife also attends.  Lots of commentators reacted as though the
vice president had revealed that he ate Irish children for dinner; personally,
I thought it was the most sensible thing I’d heard a politician say in
ages.

In fact, it reflects my own practice as a preacher.  I suppose the point can be argued that a
politician has to give equal time to everybody, but for godly men who aren’t in
positions of power (particularly for preachers), avoiding one-on-one
interaction with women offers many benefits.



**Avoiding Sin**

Of course, the first and most obvious reason why it’s wise
for Christian men never to be alone with women (who aren’t their wives, their
relatives, etc.) is because it avoids the temptation to sexual sin.  The story of the Lord’s church since the
Restoration is littered with accounts of gospel preachers who met alone with
women and ended up having affairs with them.

Along with nearly everybody else in the church, I know men
to whom this happened.  So far as I can
tell, these men generally weren’t hypocrites intending to use their position
for sexual advantage.  Instead, they
sincerely sought to serve God, yet because of their failure to take
precautions, they became entangled in sexual sin.

I can’t look at any of the men I know and say, “I’m better
than him.”  Indeed, I know I’m not.  To my shame, there have been all too many
times in my life when the devil by his cunning has led me into sin.  Thankfully, unfaithfulness to my wife has never
been one of those sins.  With humility
and fear, I mean to do everything I can to keep it that way.  If I’m never alone with another woman, I will
never betray my wife and my God with her.

I don’t think that this has to do with any characteristics
of mine in particular.  I don’t think I’m
more prone to immorality than other men, nor do I labor under the delusion that
I’m more attractive to women than they are. 
Indeed, I think the fact that I found one woman who would consent to
marry me is proof of divine intervention!

However, it isn’t only men with insatiable sex drives and
movie-star good looks who are susceptible to the wiles of the devil.  We sometimes sing the hymn, “I Know the Lord
Will Make a Way”, but I’m confident that for foolish and evil-hearted men, the
devil will make a way too.  Everyone who
wants to sin or merely fails to safeguard himself against the danger of sin
will succumb.

**Protecting
Reputations**

Sexual sin itself, though, is far from the only reason why
Christian men should avoid being alone with other women.  In fact, it’s nearly as important for us to
avoid damage to our reputations.  In 2
Corinthians 8:21, Paul writes, “We aim at what is honorable not only in the
Lord’s sight but also in the sight of men.” 
It’s important for Christians not only to do what is right, but to be
seen to do right by others.

Let’s say, for instance, that a preacher has a weekly Bible
study with a woman at her home.  Every
Thursday morning, he arrives at her house at 10 AM and leaves at 11.  It may well be that they spend every minute
of that time in earnest discussion of God’s word.  However, is that what it’s going to look like
to the neighbors?

Of course, danger to reputation doesn’t only come from third
parties.  It’s hardly unheard of for
women or men either one to make false accusations.  I know a brother who, back when he was
starting out as a young preacher, had a study with a woman and brought an older
sister of the congregation with him. 
About midway through the study, the woman said to him, “Don’t look at me
like that!”  The older sister replied,
“Young woman, he didn’t look at you like anything!”  Who knows how that story might have turned
out without the presence of an impartial third party?  I never go into a study with a woman thinking
that she’s going to slander me, but it’s reassuring to know that she can’t even
if she wants to.

**Giving Assurance**

Finally, this practice offers women the assurance that they
can meet with Christian men in safety. 
We live in a corrupt age.  Rape is
epidemic.  Men in positions of power
commonly offer women benefits in exchange for sexual favors.  In such a society, it can never be anything
other than a risk for a woman to meet alone with a man.

However, when she knows beforehand that another witness,
particularly another woman, will be present, she recognizes that she has
nothing to fear.  In my experience, when
I inform women that I’ll be studying with them with another woman (my wife or a
trusted older sister), rather than being offended, they’re grateful.  They appreciate the consideration, and they
like being able to approach the study knowing that my intentions are honorable.

**Conclusion**

The practice of a man never meeting alone with a woman (and
vice versa, though I’ve obviously never had to worry about that myself) may
seem burdensome, but in reality, it proves to be anything but.  If I have a study planned with a woman and my
wife can’t be present, it rarely takes more than a phone call or two before I
find a reputable sister who is willing to help.  In fact, they’re often glad for the
opportunity to be involved in my work.

However, this simple precaution can save Christian men a
world of suffering and heartache.  I can
think of no more elegant way to destroy absolutely everything that is important
to me than to have an affair.  I ruin my
relationship with my wife, my family, my church, and my God, and I drag
somebody else down with me in the process. 
I know people who were turned off of religion entirely by the sexual sin
of a religious leader.  What a horrible
legacy to leave behind!  When the
potential for disaster is so great, a few simple precautions (I also don’t
correspond with women except on the e-mail account I share with my wife, and
she knows she’s free to read my texts whenever she wants) are a cheap price to
pay.

I’m sure that if the trendsetters in our society had any
reason to concern themselves with what I do, they’d label me a weirdo, a
sexist, and a pervert right along with Vice President Pence.  To be blunt, I don’t care.  I want to go to heaven, and I have zero
interest in imperiling my soul for the sake of the good opinion of those who
aren’t.
