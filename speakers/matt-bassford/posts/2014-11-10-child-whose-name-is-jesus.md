*The angel said to her,
“Do not be afraid, Mary; for you have found favor with God. And behold, you
will conceive in your womb and bear a son, and you shall name Him Jesus.”*

-- Luke 1:30-31 (NASB)

Can You be the King foretold,

Child so small and lowly,

Coming forth from days of old,

Glorified and holy?

Past and future, earth and sky,

Join to sing Your lullaby,

As in Mary's arms You lie,

Child whose name is Jesus.



Now You sleep upon the earth

That was Yours in making,

Yet to magnify Your birth,

All the world is waking.

Angel  voice and
blazing star

Tell Your people where You are;

They will come from near and far,

Child whose name is Jesus.

These we treasure in our hearts

While we hear Your story,

In the light that each imparts,

We behold Your glory.

Praise to You, the Mighty One,

Sent to earth, God's only Son,

For the life that You've begun,

Child whose name is Jesus.
