Never failing nor forsaking,

God is faithful, this we know,

Like a sunrise surely breaking

Over darkness, fear, and woe.

Through temptation, doubt, and scorning,

We will wait to see His face;

As the midnight brings the morning,

So our trials bring His grace.

Never failing nor forsaking,

Though all others leave us here;

In our sleeping, in our waking,

He is certain to be near.

Like a mother, kind and caring,

How can God forget His own?

Whether joyful or despairing,

We shall never be alone.

Never failing nor forsaking,

He will guide us all our days,

By His wisdom, not mistaking

How to lead us in His ways.

In the fullness of His blessing,

Changeless through the changing years,

We will follow, still progressing

Till our blessed hope appears.
