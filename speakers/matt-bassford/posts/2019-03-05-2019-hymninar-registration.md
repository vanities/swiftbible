Registration is now open for the 2019 Hymninar.  As with all previous Hymninars, this is a week-long workshop focused on hymn lyrics.  The first half of the week will be spent analyzing hymns of enduring quality in order to determine the characteristics of a good hymn.  During the second half of the week, students will apply what they've learned.  With the help of experienced hymn writers and editors, they will be asked to write a four-line verse worthy of inclusion in a hymnal.

It is scheduled for June 17-21 in Columbia, Missouri.  Registration is $95.  This does not include travel, lodging or meals.  However, lodging is generally free, as it is provided by Christians living in the Columbia area.

If you would like to register, please use the form at the link below.  Alternatively, just contact me, and we'll figure it out somehow.  Hope to see you there!
