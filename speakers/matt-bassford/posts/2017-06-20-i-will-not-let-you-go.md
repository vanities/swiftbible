O God, abundant Giver,

I kneel before Your feet;

Your kingdom is unquestioned;

My lowliness, complete.

And yet I am determined

To taste Your grace below;

My God, unless You bless me,

I will not let You go.

At evening, when the future

Has faltered with the light,

In prayer my soul will wrestle

With You throughout the night.

And when the dawn is breaking

Upon my fear and woe,

Still then, unless You bless me,

I will not let You go.

In need of bread from heaven,

I oft approach Your throne,

Assured that Your compassion

Will not provide a stone.

Your will has filled tomorrow

With things I cannot know,

But since You’re sure to bless me,
I will not let You go.
