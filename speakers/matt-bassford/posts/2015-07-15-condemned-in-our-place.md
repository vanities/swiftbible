*By oppression and judgment he was taken away;*
*And as for his generation, who considered*
*That he was cut off out of the land of the living,*
*Stricken for the transgression of my people?*
-- Isaiah 53:8 (ESV)

A darkness now gathers that none will withstand;
A court now assembles; a trial is at hand.
The Savior from heaven, unspotted by sin,
Must face a tribunal of envious men.

Ensnared in transgression, corrupted by lust,
Our guilt is apparent; our judgment is just.
But this One is righteous!  What guilt lies in Him?
Yet judges and elders cry out and condemn.

Oppression and judgment thus take Him away,
But this we remember in wonder today.
Made holy and blameless, triumphant in grace,
We honor the Sinless, condemned in our place.
