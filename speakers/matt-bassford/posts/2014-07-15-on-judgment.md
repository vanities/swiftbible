*Have you not made
distinctions among yourselves, and become judges with evil motives?*

-- James 2:4 (NASB)

One of the great criticisms that our postmodern age levels
against Christians is that they are judgmental. 
Ironically, this is precisely untrue. 
Disciples of Jesus are the ones who make no judgments at all.  We merely repeat the judgments that our Lord
has made, and our personal tastes do not enter into our analysis.  When we stray from that ideal, when we begin
supplying “I think” in place of “It is written”, we betray Him.  It is literally not our place to judge.

 Instead, it is the worldly who are in the judgment business,
who are constantly in the process of developing and refining their think-so’s
and judging the world around them accordingly. 
They claim that they are not judgmental because they tolerate difference
in others.

In reality, many members of the secular left in our country
are every bit as intolerant as the rankest bigot in the Ozarkan backwoods.  They simply refuse to tolerate different
things, and are blithely unaware of the contradiction between their rhetoric
and their behavior.  They even use
lawsuits to compel others to live in accordance with their worldviews.  Nothing judgmental about that!
