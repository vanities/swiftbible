*In Him was life, and
the life was the Light of men.*

-- John 1:4 (NASB)

O Christ, our life and light,

O star of morning bright,

Both merciful and right

In all You do!



Arise to cheer and bless,

O Son of righteousness,

That none may know distress

Who trust in You.

When trouble year by year

Has weighed our hearts with fear,

With greater grace, appear

To make things new.

And grant that we may see

Your light eternally;

Your glory then shall be

Our glory too.
