Like many girls, my daughter is sometimes prone to. . . enthusiastic expressions of unhappiness with her parents.  Like most parents, I laugh at them.  Unlike most parents, I also convert them into song.  For instance, to the tune of the chorus to "Jesus Is Wonderful":

Daddy is horrible! 
Daddy is mean!

He is the worstest of all!

Daddy is horrible! 
Daddy is mean!

He is the worstest of all!

Sorrow and woe he will bestow,

Certain to pain and appall;

Daddy is horrible! 
Daddy is mean!

He is the worstest of all!
