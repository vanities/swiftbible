Recently, Stephen Rouse has been working on a hymn entitled "Your Body Is a Temple".  It's a thoughtful exploration of the need for personal holiness described in 1 Corinthians 6:19.  However, the last time I was reading it, I noticed it didn't have much to offer to Christians who prefer to make 1 Corinthians 6:19 about personal fitness.  I decided to help Stephen out by writing a few extra verses:


My body is a temple;

I’d better not forget

And make my temple dirty

By smoking cigarettes.

Though some find this confusing,

It’s pretty clear to me:

A temple with a smokestack

You just don’t ever see.

My body is a temple;

It’s great, but here’s the thing:

I’ve got to watch my diet

Or add another wing.

Big Macs of passing pleasure

And curly fries of sin,

The food that comes in wrappers,

I can’t let that stuff in!

My body is a temple;

There’s one thing left to prove;

In daily hardcore workouts,

I’ve got it on the move.

I’m sure to be my buffest,

Well-muscled, head to
feet,

So when I flex my biceps,
This temple’s packing heat!
