Buy this Bible if:

* You want a fairly small, easy-to-carry Bible.
* You’re looking for a translation that serves well in both
  reading and close study.
* You tolerate or even prefer double-column Bibles.
* You need at least a moderate print size for easy reading.
* You like paragraphed Bibles.
* You want a line-matched Bible.
* You’re interested in a Bible for cheap.



Don’t buy this Bible if:

* You want a cover made from actual leather, as opposed to the
  hide of the fabled Polyurethane Beast.
* You insist on single-column Bibles.
* Your eyes are bad enough that you need true large print.
* You need a Bible with a verse-by-verse format, perhaps for
  ease in preaching.
* You want cross-references. 
  This one doesn’t have ‘em.
* You don’t like Bibles with a lot of ghosting because of thin
  paper.
* You want to write in your Bible.
