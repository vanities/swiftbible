1. Speak to give grace to others, not to magnify yourself.
2. Don’t carelessly spread Internet rumors.
3. Be unfailingly polite to everyone.
4. Seek to persuade rather than to browbeat.
5. Habitually assume the best about others and their motives.
6. Make sure that the importance of a discussion justifies the
   emotional investment you are making in it.
7. Before arguing, ask yourself what you hope to accomplish.
8. If you become angry, do some serious navel-gazing before
   posting.
9. When a thread spirals out of control, stop participating in
   it.
10. Remember that you are representing Christ.
