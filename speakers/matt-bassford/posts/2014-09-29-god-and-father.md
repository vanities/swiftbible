*Blessed be the God and
Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who has blessed us with every spiritual
blessing in the heavenly places in Christ. . .*

--Ephesians 1:3 (NASB)

God and Father, by Your power,

You created all we see;

From the mountain to the flower,

Each declares Your deity.

Yet for all Your might and skill,

You regard Your creature still;

We approach You, by Your will,

As our God and Father.



God and Father, just and holy,

Throned in perfect light above,

Rich in grace, You bless the lowly,

Raising us to You in love.

Great the work that You have done:

Calling, cleansing every one,

Each, adopted as Your son,

You, our God and Father.

God and Father, wise, eternal,

Raise us to eternity,

With our mortal flesh the kernel

Of the body yet to be.

Robed in glory now unknown,

We shall gather round Your throne:

We, forevermore Your own,

You, our God and Father.
