*“Listen to the
supplications of Your servant and of Your people Israel when they pray toward
this place; hear from Your dwelling place, from heaven; hear and forgive.”*

-- 2 Chronicles 6:21 (NASB)

Hear Your church, O God!

Now Your congregation

Bows in supplication;

Hear Your church, O God!

You are great and holy,

Yet will hear the lowly;

Hear Your church, O God!



Bless Your church, O God!

Thru the woes that pain us,

May Your might sustain us;

Bless Your church, O God!

Shall Your mercies falter

Here before Your altar?

Bless Your church, O God!

Claim Your church, O God!

When this world is ending,

Hasten our ascending;

Claim Your church, O God!

Then forever take us

Where no grief can shake us;

Claim Your church, O God!
