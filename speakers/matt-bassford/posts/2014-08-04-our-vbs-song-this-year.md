I wrote this for our VBS.  My own children, at least, continue to repeat it daily.  We sang it to WEBB ("Stand Up, Stand Up, for Jesus"), but any tune of the same meter will do.  Thus:

We're traveling on to Canaan,
And God will be our guide;
With water and with manna,
Our Father will provide.
We'll learn from all the dangers
We see along the way;
They'll teach us to be humble
And always to obey.

I had great hopes for the line about always obeying.  Sadly, it does not appear to have influenced Zoë and Marky's behavior at all.
