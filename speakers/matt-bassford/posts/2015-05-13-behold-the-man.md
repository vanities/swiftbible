*Jesus then came out,
wearing the crown of thorns and the purple robe. Pilate said to them,
"Behold, the Man!"*

-- John 19:5 (NASB)

“Behold, the Man!”  A
cry of scorn

Parades Him to the crowd;

In splendid robe and crown of thorn,

He bears the shame unbowed.



“Behold, the Man!”  The
Crucified

Now lives for all to see;

His wounds, displayed in hands and side,

Reveal His victory.

“Behold, the Man!”  A
few perceive

The Lord ascend to light;

They watch until the clouds receive

Their Savior from their sight.

“Behold, the Man!” Our spirits yearn

To see that day and hour;

Your people look for Your return;

Descend, our God, with power!
