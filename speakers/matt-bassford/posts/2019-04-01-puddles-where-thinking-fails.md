They call me out upon the waters,

These half-hour songs where thinking fails,

And there I find no depth nor mystery;

These puddles dried

My mind can’t stand.



I want to call upon my brain,

But lack of substance I disdain

In puddles dried;

I need some doctrine to remain

So song can change this heart of mine.

I want to plumb the deepest waters;

The word of truth

Should be my guide,

But thinking fails when tripe surrounds me;

I long to learn, but I can’t start now.

I want to call upon my brain,

But lack of substance I disdain

In puddles dried;

I need some doctrine to remain

So song can change this heart of mine.

With not a thing to teach me.

I need truth to make me stronger;

I don’t want vain repetition!
