There is no pain nor sorrow,

Neither tears nor despairing,

In the glory tomorrow

That the Lord is preparing

In the kingdom of God.

There is no lamentation

By the banks of that river,

For it flows with salvation

From the throne of the Giver

In the kingdom of God.

There is no fear to grieve me,

Only joy past all telling,

Where the Lord will receive me;

How I long for my dwelling

In the kingdom of God!
