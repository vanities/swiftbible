Buy this Bible if:

* You’re looking for a small Bible that doesn’t have to be in
  compact territory.
* You’re a lover of edge-lined goatskin.
* You like a translation that excels for close study, even if
  it doesn’t read as smoothly as some others.
* You prefer single-column Bibles.
* You want a Bible with middle-of-the-road or a little larger
  (for Bibles) print.
* You like paragraphed Bibles.
* You want a Bible that is fully cross-referenced.
* You’re unconcerned with paper opacity.
* Line-matching is important to you.
* You don’t need wide margins to write in.
* You’re willing to pay a substantial amount of money for one
  of the highest-quality Bibles on the market.

Don’t buy this Bible if:

* You need a compact Bible.
* You are indifferent to the charms of premium covers.
* You prefer smoothness to precision in translations.
* You insist on a double-column Bible, although even here, the
  Clarion may be good enough to change your mind.
* You need a Bible with large print in the ordinary meaning of
  the word.
* You are a verse-by-verse fanatic.
* You require thick, opaque paper, even in a line-matched
  Bible.
* You’re a dedicated Bible-writer.
* You can’t afford to spend a lot of money on a Bible, no
  matter how fetching.
