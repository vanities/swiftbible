My God, with morning now beginning,

I consecrate my life anew;

Restrain my early thoughts from sinning,

And grant that they may honor You.

I give my hours into Your keeping,

My hands and heart, into Your will;

In kindness, may I be unsleeping,

That all You plan, I may fulfill.

And when the evening sun is setting,

With quiet may I greet the night,

Unmoved by worries, nor forgetting

The dawning of eternal light.
