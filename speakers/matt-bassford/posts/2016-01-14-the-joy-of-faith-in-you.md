O Lord, restore me from my sin,

Renew my troubled mind,

And help my spirit find again

The peace it left behind.

Chorus:

O Lord!  Return in righteousness,

And dwell in me anew,

For joyful heart cannot express

The joy of faith in You!

When tempted, I refused to see

That sin would have its cost;

Now You alone can comfort me

And ransom what I lost.

(Chorus)

With wisdom that I learned from pain,

I seek Your cleansing grace;

No sign of evil can remain

To mar Your dwelling place.

(Chorus)
