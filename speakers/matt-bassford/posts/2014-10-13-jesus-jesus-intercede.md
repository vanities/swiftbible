*Therefore He is able also to save forever those who draw near to God through Him, since He always lives to make intercession for them.*
-- Hebrews 7:25 (NASB)

Jesus, Jesus, intercede!
Come with love and meekness,
Faithful in the time of need,
Mindful of our weakness.
In the flesh, You suffered too,
Facing our temptation,
So for help we look to You,
Source of our salvation.

Jesus, Jesus, intercede!
Bring Your mercy near us;
From the courts of God, we plead;
Grant that He may hear us.
Great our sorrow, guilt, and shame,
Yet Your grace is greater;
Now present us in Your name,
Christ, our Mediator.

Jesus, Jesus, intercede
In the years remaining,
Through misfortune and misdeed,
Cleansing and sustaining.
Thus You evermore shall live,
Making intercession;
Lead us to the life You give,
Priest of our confession.
