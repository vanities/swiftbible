Dear MakeMusic, Inc.,

We write first of all to express
our appreciation for your Finale™ musical-notation software.  For the past twenty years, it has proven. . .
. well, not instrumental (we don’t believe in that), but very useful in our
work.  However, we have noticed a number
of inaccuracies in the way that Finale™ plays back the music we enter. Playback
does not correspond to the way that our music sounds when it is actually sung,
and this lack of correspondence causes us a great deal of difficulty.

We would like to propose a
solution.  Over time, you have added a
number of playback settings (Classical, Romantic, Jazz, etc.) to Finale™.  We believe that you also should add a **Congregational A Cappella** playback
setting.  When this setting is selected,
playback should have the following properties:



1.       One
quarter of the time, playback should occur in a key three to four steps lower
than the key in which the music is entered. 
This playback should consist only of the soprano.

2.       At any time, the first full measure of playback, along with any pickup measures, should consist
only of the soprano, one octave lower.

3.       Sixteenth
notes should become eighth notes.  Dotted
eighth notes also should become eighth notes.

4.       All
notes of durations greater than four beats should be assigned a duration of
approximately four-and-one-half beats instead. 
All four parts may be assigned a different duration.

5.       In
the three upper parts, intervals of a fourth or greater should instead cause
that part to become silent for the next measure.

6.       Soprano
and alto duets, when unaccompanied by tenor and bass, should be played back at
half speed and half volume.

7.       One
half of the time, hymns in a minor key should play back the alto and/or tenor
in the parallel major instead.

8.       Notes
above a D5 in the soprano should always be flatted.

9.       Outside
of bass leads, notes in the bass other than the first, fourth, and fifth scale
degrees (DO, FA, and SOL, respectively) should be shifted to one of those
degrees, whichever is closest.

10.    Alto
leads should consist of a series of seconds, one note half a step above, one
note half a step below, the note as written.

11.    Tenor
leads should not be played back.

12.    On
the first playback, bass leads should begin a beat later than written.  On each successive playback, they should
begin half a beat earlier than written and with doubled volume.

13.    Each
successive playback should begin at a perceptibly lower pitch and tempo than
the preceding playback.  When tempo
reaches half the rate specified in the Finale™ file, playback should pause for
a brief lecture on “following the song leader”.

All of these changes should be
simple to make, and they would add a great deal to the accuracy of your
software.  Thank you for your
consideration.  We look forward to seeing
the **Congregational A Cappella** setting
available for use in the next version of Finale™.

Sincerely,

The hymnists of the churches of
Christ
