Just as I am, and with one plea:

O God, don’t ask so much from me!

It’s got no point that I can see;

O Lamb of God, I come! 
I come!



Just as I am, without a doubt

That I am what this thing’s about,

So put me first, or else I’m out!

O Lamb of God, I come! 
I come!

Just as I am, untaught and proud,

So talk of sin is not allowed

(It puts me under quite a cloud);

O Lamb of God, I come! 
I come!

Just as I am, averse to pain,

So let me make my purpose plain:

Just as I am, I will remain;

O Lamb of God, I come! 
I come!
