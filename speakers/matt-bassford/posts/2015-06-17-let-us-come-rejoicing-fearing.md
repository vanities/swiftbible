Let us come, rejoicing, fearing,

To the feast the Lord has set.

In the bread and cup appearing,

Priest and sacrifice are met.

Now recalling and revering,

We His saints will not forget.

Chosen from among the races,

Israel came to hear the Law;

Oft they fell upon their faces,

Crushed by guilt for every flaw;

As we share these greater graces,

Let us show a greater awe.

Of the mystery partaking,

Sharing in the broken bread,

By His mercy, we are taking

Life and healing from the dead.

May we live without forsaking

Christ, our Savior and our Head!
