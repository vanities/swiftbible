(Note:  because my
Bible is looong since out of print, all of this refers to the Star Bible
facsimile reprint)

Buy this Bible if. . .

* You don’t mind a Bible with substantial girth.
* You’re willing to pay a substantial sum for a book block
  that will have to be rebound if you want a nice cover.
* You want to use what is probably the most precise
  translation that will ever exist in English. 
  Well, in “English”.
* You’re fine with double column.
* You read moderately sized print just fine.
* You like your Bibles paragraphed, although some of the
  paragraphs in this setting are so long that the Bible looks like it was
  formatted by an Internet ranter.
* You want a truly massive cross-reference suite.
* You like Bibles with perfectly opaque paper.  Both the Nelson originals and the Star
  reprints have this, actually.
* You don’t aim to write in the margins much.
* You’re so determined to have an ASV that you’re willing to
  drop $100 on one, plus shipping.  There
  is no discount off retail on this Bible because it is only available directly
  from Star.

Don’t buy this Bible if. . .

* You want a little Bible.
* You don’t like bonded leather, and you don’t want to fool
  with the time and expense of a rebind either.
* You require at least some semblance of readability in your
  Bibles.  “Archaic English words in Greek
  syntax” is not far off as a description of the ASV.  I say this as one who loves the ASV and is
  periodically tempted to adopt it as my primary translation.
* For you, it’s single column or bust.
* You need a large-print Bible.
* Only verse-by-verse will do for you.  Sadly, the only verse-by-verse ASV I’ve ever
  seen is an out-of-print edition by the also-defunct High Village
  Publishers.  People on Amazon want around
  $275 for it, even though the ASV text it uses is fairly corrupted.
* You don’t like cross-references.
* You’d rather have a skinny Bible than one with opaque paper.
* You want to write lots and lots in the margins.  The only wide-margin ASV’s I’ve heard of are
  the extortionate High Village verse-by-verse Bible mentioned above and an old
  Nelson wide-margin that will set you back a pretty penny on eBay.
* You think that $100 for a bonded leather Bible is exorbitant
  and not to be considered under any circumstances.
