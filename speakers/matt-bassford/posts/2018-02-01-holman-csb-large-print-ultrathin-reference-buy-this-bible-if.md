Buy this Bible if:

* You want an edge-lined Bible with a luscious cover.
* You’re interested in trying a translation that reads more
  simply but retains the original meaning.
* You are OK with double-column Bibles.
* You like Bibles in paragraphed format.
* You want a Bible with a robust reference suite.
* You appreciate line-matching in a Bible.
* You want a premium-quality Bible for (comparatively) cheap.

Don’t buy this Bible if:

* You’re insistent on a word-for-word translation.
* You prefer single-column Bibles.
* Your close-in eyesight is terrible.
* You require a Bible that is verse-by-verse.
* Only super-duper-opaque paper will do.
* You want a Bible with wide margins for writing in.
* You’re more concerned with low price than comparative value
  (though this setting is available in much cheaper formats too).
