A single sheep, a single sheep,

The shepherd goes to find a single sheep.

How his neighbors will rejoice

For the sake of a single sheep!

A single coin, a single coin,

The woman searches for a single coin.

How her neighbors will rejoice

For the sake of a single coin!

A single son, a single son,

The father watches for a single son.

How his household will rejoice

For the sake of a single son!

A single soul, a single soul,

Our Father watches for a single soul.

How His angels will rejoice

For the sake of a single soul!
