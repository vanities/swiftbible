For her Bible class this quarter, my five-year-old daughter, Zoë, was asked to compose her own psalm.  Here it is:

I love you, God, yes I do!
Yes I do, I love you, Lord!
'Cause You're my Savior,
Yes, You are!

It's really quite thoughtful.  For a five-year-old.
