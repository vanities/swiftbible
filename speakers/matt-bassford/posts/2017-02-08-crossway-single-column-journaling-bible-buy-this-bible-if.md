Buy this Bible if:

* You want one of the smaller wide-margin Bibles out there.
* You want a journaling Bible in genuine leather (though here,
  be sure to turn to christianbook.com).
* You want a translation that’s equally good for reading and
  close study.
* You prefer or are at least indifferent to single-column
  Bibles..
* You like your Bibles in paragraphed format.
* You don’t need more than a few minimal cross-references.
* You want a Bible with decent paper.
* Line-matching in a Bible is useful to you.
* You need lots of room in the margin to write.
* You’re willing to pay for a nicer mass-market Bible.



Don’t buy this Bible if:

* You need a Bible you can put in a pocket.
* You demand a Bible sheathed in the skin of goats or calves.
* It’s double-column or nothing for you.
* Your eyes require a larger-print Bible.
* You insist on verse-by-verse format.
* You want a full cross-reference suite.
* You require super-duper paper.
* You think around $45 is too much for a Bible.
