Buy this Bible if:

* You’re interested in a Bible of moderate size and weight.
* You’re OK with a pretty flimsy polyurethane cover.
* You want a translation that isn’t the best for close study,
  but that reads extremely smoothly.
* You prefer or at least can tolerate double-column Bibles.
* You can handle print that is moderately sized, at least for
  a Bible.
* You like paragraphed Bibles.
* You don’t particularly want a Bible with a full
  cross-reference suite.
* You are indifferent to paper-opacity concerns.
* You don’t care about line-matching either.
* You don’t want to write in the Bible.
* You’re looking to pick up an NLT for cheap, cheap, cheap.

Don’t buy this Bible if:

* You need a Bible small enough to fit in a purse, etc.
* You only want a Bible with a cover made from the skin of a
  dead animal.
* You want a Bible with a precise translation, even if it’s
  harder to understand.
* You insist on single-column Bibles.
* You need or prefer a verse-by-verse Bible.
* You require lots and lots of cross-references.
* You can’t stand paper with lots of show-through.
* It’s line-matching or bust for you.
* You want to write in it.
