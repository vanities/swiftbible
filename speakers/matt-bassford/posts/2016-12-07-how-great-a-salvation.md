"How great a salvation!" we sing in elation;

All glory to God for the work He has done!

Before the beginning, foreknowing our sinning,

He chose us for mercy in Jesus His Son.

The Christ in His meekness died once for our weakness;

Now all of us live by the gift of His grace.

Raised up to perfection by His resurrection,

We gratefully serve Him and seek for His face.

How great a salvation despite tribulation!

We cherish a hope that no grief can destroy:

His power will hide us, His faithfulness, guide us,

His wisdom, complete us and bring us to joy.

In sorrow and trouble His blessings redouble,

For no other help is as sure as the Lord!

By our perseverance until His appearance,

We follow His footsteps and gain His reward.

How great a salvation to share with each nation,

To carry to regions where no one has heard!

Here too, may the grieving be led to believing

And hearts that are hardened be pierced by the word!

Not fearing to name Him, may we so proclaim Him:

Declaring His wonders both near and abroad,

Retelling the story of suffering and glory

Till all men are saved by the kindness of God!
