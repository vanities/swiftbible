Hear my
righteous cause, O Lord!

Give
attention to my cries;

From Your
presence, send my help;

Keep my plight
before Your eyes.

Test me, and
find nothing wrong,

For my mouth
does not transgress;

Taught
according to Your word,

I have
walked in righteousness.



Saving God,
incline Your ear;

Hear my
pleading when I speak;

Wondrous in
Your steadfast love,

Grant the
refuge that I seek.

As the apple
of Your eye,

Hide me with
Your wings outspread

From the
violence of the foes

Who surround
my soul with dread.

Pitiless,
they close their hearts,

Swift as
lions to the strife;

Keep me from
such men, O Lord,

Who delight
in earthly life.

Wealth and
children are their hope,

And You
offer these with grace,

Yet I will be satisfied

Waking to
behold Your face.
