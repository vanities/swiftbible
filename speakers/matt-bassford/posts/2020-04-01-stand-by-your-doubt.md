Sometimes it’s hard to be a skeptic,

Giving up on hope to feed your doubt,

You know your bad times

Won’t lead to good times;

But you still don’t wanna be devout;

If you’d love Jesus, He’d forgive you,

Even though you’ve acted like a lout;

But you won’t love Him or believe in Him,

‘Cause after all, you’d rather doubt.

Stand by your doubt;

It gives you all your reasons

To keep on disobeyin’;

Though life is cold and lonely,

Stand by your doubt,

Your one excuse to cling to

Or else you’ll have to do without;

Stand by your doubt;

Stand by your doubt,

Your one excuse to cling to

Or else you’ll have to do without,

Stand by your doubt!
