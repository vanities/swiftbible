Oh, I want to shoot him
In his groundhog face
With a 12-gauge shotgun,
Ending his disgrace.
In the city limits,
Let me fire my gun;
Rodent past, dead at last--
Such a lot of fun!

Tune:  "Oh, I Want to See Him"
