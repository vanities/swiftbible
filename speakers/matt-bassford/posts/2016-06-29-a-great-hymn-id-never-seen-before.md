Yesterday, when I put [this post](http://hisexcellentword.blogspot.com/2016/06/what-makes-for-great-congregational.html) up on a Facebook group dedicated to church music, a commenter expressed both his love of congregational singing and his love of a hymn I'd never heard of before.  This is unusual; generally, whenever anybody expresses their love of some congregational hymn, I will know it even if it's not part of the repertoire of the churches of Christ.  Most hymnists are always on the lookout for quality hymns; it's a lot easier finding them than writing them!


I looked the hymn up online and was immediately struck by the quality of its lyrics and its particular usefulness for my religious tradition.  I don't think I've ever seen a text better suited for use as a baptismal hymn.  When paired with the exuberant hymn tune AFFECTION, "O Boundless Salvation" is a worship powerhouse!

The author is William Booth, better known as the founder of the Salvation Army, and in Salvationist circles, this is known as "The Founder's Hymn".  [Here's what it sounds like](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wcEXwZ6EGI8) when the Salvationists use it in their worship.

I've met AFFECTION before (in *Psalms, Hymns, and Spiritual Songs,* it's offered as an alternative tune to "How Firm a Foundation"), and in my opinion, no a-cappella church should take it as slowly as the Salvationists do.  They have drums, etc., to add interest to what is a very stable harmony in some places.  As a result, when I set it, I set it in 2/2 instead of 4/4 so that it will move faster.

"O Boundless Salvation!" was written in the 1880's, which makes it public-domain.  Nobody needs to ask my permission (or anyone else's) to take, copy, use, publish, etc.  I hope people will do so.  This is a hymn that the churches of Christ ought to be singing.
