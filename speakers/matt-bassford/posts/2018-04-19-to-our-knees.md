O God, You bring us to our knees,

For You are very great;

We marvel at the mysteries

You purpose and create.

The starry reaches of the night

Declare Your majesty and might;

They show You clearly to our sight

And bring us to our knees.



O God, You bring us to our knees

Before Your perfect law;

How wonderful are Your decrees!

We read each one with awe.

For us, Your judgments all display

The hidden wisdom of Your way;

When we are faithful to obey,

They bring us to our knees.

O God, You bring us to our knees,

For You have shown us grace.

In kindness, You have heard our pleas

And freed our hopeless race.

For all that You have done and do,

O God, what can we say to You?

Each day, You make Your mercies new

And bring us to our knees.
