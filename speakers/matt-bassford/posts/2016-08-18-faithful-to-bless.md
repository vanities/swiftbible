Faithful to Bless

Give thanks to the Lord of creation

Whose goodness and kindness endure;

Be glad in the hope of salvation

Because His redemption is sure.

Chorus:

We cried to the Lord in our trouble;

He rescued us from our distress,

So now let His praises redouble

Because He is faithful to bless!

The counsel of God we rejected;

We sat in the bondage of sin;

With grace that restored and perfected,

He led us to freedom again.

(chorus)

As sailors upon a great ocean,

We trembled in fear at the waves,

He banished the storm and commotion--

A God who still listens and saves.



(chorus)
