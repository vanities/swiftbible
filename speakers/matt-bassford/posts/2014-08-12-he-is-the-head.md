My favorite quatrain in any German hymn is the second half of the first verse of "Herz und Herz, Vereint Zusammen".  It is so perfectly written that I do not believe it is possible to fully capture it in English rhyme and meter.  However, the thoughts it contains are beautiful and powerful on their own.  Concerning Jesus, the hymn says:

He is the Head; we are His members.
He is the light; we are its gleam.
He is the master; we are the brothers.
He is ours; we are His.

May each of you know today the perfect peace of unity with Jesus Christ!
