Buy this Bible if. . .

* You like your Bibles like you like your paychecks—big.
* You’re OK with a handsome-looking polyurethane cover.
* You want a translation that’s a decent all-rounder, maybe
  slanted a little toward specializing in close study.
* You prefer double-column Bibles.
* You can easily read anything but fine print.
* You’re looking for a verse-by-verse Bible.  In fact, in the ESV, this is your only choice
  at the moment.
* You want a full cross-reference suite.
* You want a Bible with paper that’s fairly opaque.
* You’re indifferent to the presence of line-matching.
* You don’t want to write a whole lot in the margins.
* You’re willing to pay a little more to get a bigger Bible.

Don’t buy this Bible if. . .

* You want/need a smaller Bible that’s very easy to stow and
  carry.
* You demand that your Bible be clothed in the skins of dead
  animals.
* You’re a single-column devotee.
* You need a larger-print Bible.
* You don’t see why anyone would ever break out the text of
  the Bible verse by verse.  Paragraphs all
  the way for you!
* You demand super-opaque paper, like in a Cambridge
  Wide-Margin.
* You refuse to use a Bible that isn’t line-matched.
* You want margins large enough to draw or write novels in.
* You can’t afford anything more than a bargain-basement
  Bible.
