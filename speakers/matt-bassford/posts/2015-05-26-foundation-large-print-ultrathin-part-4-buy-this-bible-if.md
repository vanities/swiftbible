Buy this Bible if. . .

* You prefer, or at least don’t mind, a larger Bible of
  moderate weight.
* You want the ultra-flexible cover that only an edge-lined
  Bible can possess.
* You want a translation that excels for close study.
* You like or tolerate double-column Bibles.
* Your eyes aren’t absolutely terrible for reading.
* You want a Bible in verse-by-verse format.
* You want a Bible with a full suite of cross-references.
* You’re OK with Bible paper that isn’t terribly opaque.
* You’re indifferent to whether your Bible is line-matched.
* You need some space for writing in the margin of your Bible,
  but you don’t intend to put a novel there.
* You’re willing to pay for a premium Bible, but don’t want to
  soar into the stratosphere of high-premium Bibles.

Don’t buy this Bible if. . .

* You want a small Bible.
* You don’t like a super-flexible cover enough to pay three
  times as much for it.
* You want a translation that is optimized for smooth reading.
* You insist on having a single-column Bible.
* You need a magnifying glass to read a banner headline in a
  newspaper.  If you don’t know what a
  newspaper is, this probably does not apply to you.
* You require a Bible in paragraphed format.
* You’d rather have a text Bible.
* You’d rather not be able to read Ezekiel when you have your
  Bible open to Genesis.
* You think line-matching is important.
* You intend to preserve the totality of your Biblical
  understanding for the next generation in the margins of your Bible.
* You can’t imagine spending more than the price of a pair of
  shoes on a Bible (though much cheaper versions of this Bible are available).
