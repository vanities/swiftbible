O Father, bless us all

Before we leave this place,

For every saint will surely fall

Without Your guiding grace.

O Father, bless us all;

In mercy, hear our need;

The way is hard, our strength is small,

But Yours is great indeed.

O Father, bless us all;

May all our days be blessed

Until we hear Your trumpet call
Or pass into Your rest.
