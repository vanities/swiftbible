The last couple of months have been complicated.  Changing churches, selling one house, buying another, packing up all of my possessions (including 50 boxes of books—ugh!) moving them 500 miles, and then unpacking them has proven to be every bit as demanding and time-consuming as I expected it to be.  I’m thankful for the change (which now has me working with the Jackson Heights church of Christ in Columbia, TN), but it did require me to cut everything non-essential out of my life.

One of the casualties was blogging.  I didn’t even have the time to write posts, much less reply to others.  I’ve missed it, but, well, in the face of 50 boxes of books to be boxed up, that’s how things had to be.

However, even though I’m not entirely out of the woods yet (I’m really looking forward to all the bureaucratic hoops I’ll have to jump through to establish myself as a resident of Tennessee!), I can at least see light glimmering between the trees.  That means I’m able to start blogging again.

Some things are going to remain the same.  All of my sermon manuscripts will continue to appear online (though now there are also going to be recordings available on the Jackson Heights website, thebibleway.org).  So too will hymn lyrics and hymn-theory ponderings.  However, I think the time of the Bible review has come to an end, although there are a few last reviews in the pipeline.

Also, as part of my work at Jackson Heights, I’m going to be maintaining the church blog, withgodsword.com, and I’m sure that many of the posts from With God’s Word (a little more sermon-like in tone) will make their way over to His Excellent Word too.  We’ll just have to see how things go.

Regardless, apologies to everybody out there who was waiting for more HEW posts and spent the past six weeks being disappointed!  If the Lord wills, I’ll be able to avoid such disruptions for years to come.  For now, though, thanks for reading!  If there’s anything you want me to write about, just holler.
