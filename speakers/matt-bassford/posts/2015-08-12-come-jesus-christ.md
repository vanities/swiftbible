This one started about eight months ago, when I was tagged on Facebook by Sheri Mauck, a sister in Christ and a longtime friend of mine.  She posted part of a prayer by 17th-century Russian monk Dmitri of Rostov and wrote, "I need you to make this a song, 'k?"  The full prayer goes like this:

*Open, o doors and bolts of my heart, that Christ the King of Glory may enter!*

*Enter, O my Light, and enlighten my darkness;*
*Enter, O my Life, and resurrect my deadness;*
*Enter, O my Physician, and heal my wounds;*
*Enter, O Divine Fire, and burn up the thorns of my sins;*
*Ignite my inward parts and my heart with the flame of Thy love;*
*Enter, O my King, and destroy in me the kingdom of sin;*
*Sit on the throne of my heart and reign in me alone,*
*O Thou, my King and my Lord!*

I looked at what she had posted and quickly concluded that there was a hymn there.  Eight months later, I've arrived at a working draft.


Come, Jesus Christ, within my darkness shining;
With truth, dispel my night;
Illuminate my soul, its depths defining,
O Jesus Christ, my light!

Come, Jesus Christ, for lust and sin have slain me,
And mend the wounds of strife,
Then by Your breath revive, by grace sustain me,
O Jesus Christ, my life!

Come, Jesus Christ; burn up the thorns of sinning
And turn my heart above;
Restore the joy I knew at the beginning,
O Jesus Christ, my love!

Come, Jesus Christ, and conquer in me wholly;
Destroy each wicked thing,
Then claim my captive heart and reign there solely,
O Jesus Christ, my King!
