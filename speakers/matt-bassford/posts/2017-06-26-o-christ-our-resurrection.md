O Christ, our resurrection,

O Christ, our life,

Our peace and our protection,

Our shield in strife,

We come in faith, receiving

Your firm reply:

That we who live, believing,

Shall never die.

This living hope we nourish

Who linger here,

For like the grass, we flourish,

Then disappear.

Your throne cannot be shaken;

Your word is true,

So all who sleep shall waken,

Alive in You.

O Christ, our resurrection,

O Christ, who rose,

Compel the last subjection

Of all Your foes.

Then rise, Your reign completed,

And yield Your seat,

For death shall lie defeated

Beneath Your feet.
