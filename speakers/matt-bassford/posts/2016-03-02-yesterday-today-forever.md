Yesterday, today, forever,

Jesus Christ is still the same;

He was faithful to deliver
Holy men of old from shame.
Like a shield to His anointed,

How He blessed them all their days!

They were never disappointed,

And they still declare His praise.

Yesterday, today, forever,

Jesus Christ is still the same,

Now He blesses our endeavor

When we call upon His name.

Come with prayer, you weak and humble,

While it still is called “Today”,

For our feet can never stumble

Where the Lord prepares the way.

Yesterday, today, forever,

Jesus Christ is still the same;

With us always, He will never

Fail to grant the aid we claim.

For His grace and His protection,

For His love unto the end,

For our hope of resurrection,

Praise our Savior, Guide, and Friend!
