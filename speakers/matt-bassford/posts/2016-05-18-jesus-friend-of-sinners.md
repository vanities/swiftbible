Jesus, Friend of sinners,

Holy and divine,

Join us at our table;

Condescend to dine.

We are weak and helpless,

Longing to be fed;

Fill our hungry spirits

With Your living bread.

Jesus, Friend of sinners,

Help us know our sin;

Pierce our self-deception;

Shine Your light within.

With the poor in spirit

May we find our place,

Fit to share the kingdom

Only by Your grace.

Jesus, Friend of sinners,

May we learn from You;

As You grant forgiveness,

Teach us mercy too.

Fill our earthen vessels

With Your boundless love

Till we help the sinner

Join Your friends above.
