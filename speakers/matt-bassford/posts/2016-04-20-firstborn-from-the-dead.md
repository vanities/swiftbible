Come with Salome and Mary,

Through the early gloom;

Seek the place where Christ was buried;

Find an empty tomb.

Hear the angel, dread and glorious:

"See where He was laid;

"He is risen, now victorious;

“Do not be afraid."

Leave with fear and joy together;

Tell what he has said;

Hail the Lamb who lives forever,

Firstborn from the dead.
