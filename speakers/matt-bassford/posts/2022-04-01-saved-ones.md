My heart cannot agree

To sing in any song

Of heaven with those dear to me

When they might not belong.

If they should misbehave

Before that rest above,

I will not love the ones He saves,

Nor save the ones I love.

It’s better to rewrite

So that I can prepare

By singing of my home in light

Without a “loved one” there.

With ransomed wretches, then,

I’ll spend eternity;

My loved ones they have never been,

But “saved ones” they shall be.

*Suggested tune:  "Loved Ones"*
