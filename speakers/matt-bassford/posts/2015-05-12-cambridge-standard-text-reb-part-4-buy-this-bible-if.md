Buy this Bible if:

* You want a Bible of moderate size.
* You’d be willing to put up with a polyurethane cover (with a
  cool design on it).
* You want a Bible that’s better suited for reading than for
  close study, but with a higher tone than the NLT, for instance.
* You are willing to tolerate double-column Bibles.
* Your eyes are good enough to handle the smallish print that
  is considered “Standard” in Bibles.
* You prefer Bibles in which every verse is not its own paragraph.
* You want a Bible that is text-only.
* You’re fine with paper with moderate opacity (again, for
  Bibles)
* You aren’t concerned about whether your Bible is
  line-matched.
* You don’t want to write in the margins much at all.
* You’re willing to pay about twice what a poly NIV would run
  to get an unusual translation.

Don’t buy this Bible if:

* You need a Bible you can fit in a coat pocket.
* You expect your Bible covers to be made from dead animals.
* You’re looking for a translation well suited for close
  study.
* You’re a single-column fanatic.
* Only large-print Bibles will do for you.
* You want a verse-by-verse Bible.
* You want a Bible with a cross-reference suite.
* Only Bibles with the most opaque paper suit you.
* You demand line-matching.
* You want a Bible with expansive margins for taking lots of
  notes in.
* You want a Bible, any Bible, and cost is a major concern.
