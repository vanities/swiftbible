High in heaven, throned in light
Reigns a holy King,
Greater than the pen can write
Or the tongue can sing.
Vainly do the angels cry
Praises day and night;
None can fitly glorify
Nor declare His might.

Perfect as a sea of glass
Is His purity,
Where the sinner cannot pass
Nor the wicked be.
Who would dare behold His face,
Shining like the sun?
Who would seek that holy place
Or the Holy One?

Not with trembling nor in vain
Do we near His throne;
By the Lamb that once was slain,
We are made His own.
Holy, blameless, justified,
We approach Him there,
For the grace He will provide
When we come with prayer.
