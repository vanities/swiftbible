Before the light of God first shone,

Before the earth was formed in place,

He made a plan to save His own,

To reconcile our fallen race.

He knew the price that He must pay

To bring the sons of earth above:

He sent His only Son away,

The sacrifice compelled by love.

His fate ordained before His birth,

In human form, the Chosen came;

Between the heavens and the earth,

He hung in agony and shame.

The tombs were opened at His cry

And darkness draped the skies above;

For God had sent His Son to die,

And die He did, compelled by love.

That silent body on the cross

Still speaks in eloquent decree:

“For you, I suffered every loss;

“Now you in turn must die for Me.”

Before that mute command, we bow

And daily seek the things above;

Denying all but Jesus now,

We live for Him, compelled by love.
