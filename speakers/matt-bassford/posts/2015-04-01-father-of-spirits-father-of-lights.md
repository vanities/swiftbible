*Furthermore, we had
earthly fathers to discipline us, and we respected them; shall we not much
rather be subject to the Father of spirits, and live?*--Hebrews 12:9 (NASB)

Father of spirits, Father of lights,
Gracious and faithful, loving and right;
Ruler of heaven, rise from Your throne;
Come with Your kindness; honor Your own.

Father of spirits, Father of lights,
Conquer our blindness; strengthen our sight.
Know our distractions; banish each one.
Bless us with vision fixed on Your Son.

Father of spirits, Father of lights,
Waken our mercies; keep us from spite.
Bind us together; guide us above,
Patient in spirit, perfect in love.
