Above the throne of God on high,

Two seraphim in splendor fly,

And each to each, they raise the cry,

“The Lord of
hosts is holy!”

With wings, they cover up their feet

Lest they should tread before His seat,

And by this reverence, they repeat,

“The Lord of
hosts is holy!”

Two wings conceal each seraph head

Before His majesty and dread,

And so three times the word is spread,

“The Lord of
hosts is holy!”

May we take part in their refrain

And serve without reproach or stain

Till word and deed both make it plain:

“The Lord of
hosts is holy!”
