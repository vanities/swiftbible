*But the Jerusalem
above is free; she is our mother.*

-- Galatians 4:26 (NASB)

On earth, I am a stranger;

While in this tent, I groan;

I seek through toil and danger

A city of my own.

Her gates are twelve in number;

Her walls are high and broad;

Her watchmen never slumber;

Her architect is God.



Chorus:

O rest for pilgrims given!

O city of my love!

Eternal in the heavens,

Jerusalem above!

I shall not be returning

To where I dwelt before;

My soul cries out with yearning;

My spirit longs for more.

A distant land has claimed me;

My hope has rested there,

Where God Himself has named me

Both citizen and heir.

(Chorus)

When darkness looms before me

And life is nearly spent,

I’ll whisper on the journey

The song of my ascent,

And then I’ll hear in wonder

Its answer in that hymn

That swells as loud as thunder

Within Jerusalem.

(Chorus)
