Buy this Bible if:


* You want a hardback Bible to give you a writing surface for journaling in your lap.
* You prefer a translation that’s equally good for reading and close study.
* You like double-column Bibles.
* You prefer paragraphed Bibles.
* You don’t need more than a few of the most useful references.
* You want a Bible with some of the thickest, most opaque paper I’ve ever seen in a Bible.
* You like line-matching.
* You want more white space to write/draw in than any other Bible on the market offers.
* You’re not looking to spend a lot of money.

Don’t buy this Bible if:


* You’re looking for a small, easy-to-carry edition.
* You insist on a Bible covered in the skin of a dead animal.
* You want either a super-easy reader or a Bible that excels at close study.
* You’re a single-column fan.
* You prefer verse-by-verse Bibles.
* You want a full cross-reference suite with tens of thousands of references.
