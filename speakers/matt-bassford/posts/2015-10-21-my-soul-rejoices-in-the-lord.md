My soul rejoices in the Lord,

And of His greatness I will sing;

With life renewed and joy restored,

I come with song before the King.



My soul rejoices in His grace,

The lovingkindness He has shown;

With saints from every land and race,

I come with thanks before His throne.

My soul rejoices in His word,

So rich with promise and reward;

How could my spirit not be stirred?

I come with hope before the Lord.
