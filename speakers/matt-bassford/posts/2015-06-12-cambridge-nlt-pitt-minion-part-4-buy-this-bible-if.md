Buy this Bible if. . .

* You want a small Bible
* You’re looking for a cover that is more durable than poly
  but not as pricey as goatskin.
* You want to use what is probably the easiest-to-understand
  Bible that is still a translation.  Don’t
  expect precision in close study here, though.
* You like or at least tolerate double-column Bibles.
* You can read small print easily.
* You like your Bibles paragraphed.
* You want the most extensive reference suite the NLT has to
  offer.
* You can handle semi-translucent paper.
* You would like a Bible that is mostly, though not entirely,
  line-matched.
* You don’t intend to write in the margins much.
* You’re willing to pay a premium to get a premium Bible.



Don’t buy this Bible if. . .

* You like big Bibles for some reason.
* Nothing but sumptuous goatskin will do for you.
* You want to use a translation that renders the Hebrew and
  Greek texts at a word-for-world level.
* You need reading glasses.
* You prefer verse-by-verse layouts.
* Larger print is more important to you than cross-references.
* You require fairly opaque paper.
* Even small problems with line-matching drive you buggo.
* You would like to write extensively in the margins (and
  neither Cambridge nor anybody else appears to publish a wide-margin NLT, so
  you’re really out of luck here).
* You can’t imagine paying $100 retail for something that
  weighs about a pound.
