*But put on the Lord
Jesus Christ, and make no provision for the flesh in regard to its lusts.*

--Romans 13:14

O Jesus, may my days begin

When I put on Your truth within;

By meditation, may I gird

My understanding with Your word.



Then shall Your power and grace be free

To clothe me with humility,

Till word and action both express

Your patience and Your lowliness.

Then lastly, may I put on love,

Greatest of all the things above;

It joins my brother, You, and me,

The perfect bond of unity.

So clothed like You, may I proclaim

The excellencies of Your name

And by a blameless life, declare

Your sovereign glories everywhere.
