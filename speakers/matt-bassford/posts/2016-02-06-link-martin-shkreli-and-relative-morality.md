I thought [this blog post](http://www.intellectualtakeout.org/blog/martin-shkreli-if-morality-relative-why-are-we-outraged) was a short but (and?) worthwhile read.  In addition to the issues raised by the author, a couple of others suggest themselves:

- This looks awfully like the beginning of the generalized moral failure at the end of Romans 1.

- It reveals the foolishness of the idea that legality can ever stand in for consensus morality.  Shkreli found a loophole and exploited it.  Now he can smirk at everybody who is outraged at him for being a bad guy.  The only way to keep people from evil is to convince them to follow an objective system of morality, and good luck establishing one of those without religion!  Evil people who aren't smart will break the law without concern for it, and evil people who are smart will find loopholes.
