God of the broken, heal my heart,

Shattered with grief and sin,

Nor let Your care and grace depart

Once I am whole again.

God of the weeping, hear my cry;

Answer with love, I pray.

From Your compassion, make reply;

Wipe every tear away.

God of the friendless, o! be near;

Come, though I cannot see.

Even through loneliness and fear,

Never abandon me.

God of the dying, take my hand

When all my griefs are past,

Lead me to glory in that land;

Bring me to peace at last.
