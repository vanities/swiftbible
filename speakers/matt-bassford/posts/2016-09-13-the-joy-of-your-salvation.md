Restore to me the joy of Your salvation,

O God, my trust;

Pour out Your grace; withhold Your indignation,

For I am dust.

Restore to me the joy of Your salvation;

Forget my sin;

Recall my heart from error and temptation

To You again.

Restore to me the joy of Your salvation;

Bestow Your peace;

In love, rebuke my grief and agitation,

And make them cease.

Restore to me the joy of Your salvation;

Regard my prayer:

Prepare a place not part of this creation;

Receive me there.
