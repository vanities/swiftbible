*The woman conceived;
and she sent and told David, and said, "I am pregnant."*

-- 2 Samuel 11:5 (NASB)

Who knows how many millions of
men have heard just the same words that David did?  How many have had the same sinking
feeling as he did when they too realized it wasn’t over?  David thought he was through with Bathsheba
when she went back to her house, but the only thing that was over was the
pleasure of sin.  Now, he had to deal
with the consequences of adultery.



Sin works the same way in our
lives.  Seeking joy through sin is
like going on a spending spree with a credit card.  You get the fun now, but you have to pay
later, and you keep on paying and paying, far beyond the worth of what you have
received.

There are two reasons why this
is so.  The first is the nature of sin,
and the second is the nature of Satan.  We must not forget that sin is sin because it is bad for
us.  God didn’t forbid fun things.  He forbade things that look fun but in
reality are self-destructive.

Second, we also must remember that
Satan’s goal is to take over our lives and destroy us spiritually.  When we give into sin, we open a crack in our
defenses.  Satan’s not going to hit us
where we’re strong.  He’s going to
exploit our weakness, and keep on hitting us there until he succeeds in killing
us.  The fun stops, but the results keep
going.
