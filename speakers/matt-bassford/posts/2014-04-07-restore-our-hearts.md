*Restore us to You, O
LORD, that we may be restored; Renew our days as of old.*

-- Lamentations 5:21 (NASB)

Restore our hearts to You, O Lord;

Restore, that we may be restored.

The love of many has grown cold;

Renew our days, as days of old.

With outstretched arm and mighty hand,

Reclaim us from this foreign land.

 Restore our minds to hear Your law,

To learn in lowliness and awe.

Expose the darkness of our sin,

Until You break our wills again.

With flesh abased and spirit stirred,

We all shall tremble at Your word.

Restore our souls as we repent;

Confirm Your holy covenant.

Possess this people for Your own;

Engrave our hearts like precious stone.

Inscribe our call and our reward:

"A nation holy to the Lord."
