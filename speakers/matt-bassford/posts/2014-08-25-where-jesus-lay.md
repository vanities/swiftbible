*But Mary was standing outside the tomb weeping; and so, as she wept, she stooped and looked into the tomb; and she saw two angels in white sitting, one at the head and one at the feet, where the body of Jesus had been lying.*
-- John 20:11-12 (NASB)

To the place where Jesus lay
Women come at early day,
Bringing spices through the gloom
For the body in the tomb.
Then with terror and delight
They behold an awesome sight,
For the Holy One has gone
With the breaking of the dawn.

In the place where Jesus lay
Angels wait in bright array
As the cherubim of old
Stood beside an ark of gold.
Seated still at head and feet,
They reveal the mercy seat,
Where a God with unseen face
Greets His people with His grace.

To the place where Jesus lay,
We direct our hearts today;
Not with fragrant oil nor spice
Do we seek the Sacrifice.
Now we know His tomb to be
Emptied by His victory;
Day by day, our joy remains,
For the Savior lives and reigns.
