Buy this Bible if:

* You prefer or at least don’t mind large Bibles.
* You’re OK with a hardback Bible or you simply plan to have
  it rebound.
* You want a translation that excels for close study, even at
  the cost of being easy to read.
* Double-column Bibles are fine by you.
* Your eyes aren’t terrible for reading.
* You have no objections to verse-by-verse Bibles.
* You don’t need a Bible with a cross-reference suite.
* You want a Bible with truly excellent paper.
* You are indifferent to line-matching in a Bible with truly
  excellent paper.
* You want a wide-margin Bible.
* You’re willing to pay $40 retail (maybe a little less than
  $30 online) for a hardback Bible.

Don’t buy this Bible if:

* You need a small Bible.
* You want a Bible with a leather or at least a poly cover.
* You want a translation that flows easily, even at the cost
  of word-for-word precision.
* You prefer single-column Bibles.
* You need a Bible with Great Big Print.
* You think verse-by-verse Bibles break up the text too much.
* You can’t imagine using a Bible without cross-references.
* You think even a Bible with truly excellent paper ought to
  be line-matched.
* You think $40 for a hardback is excessive.
