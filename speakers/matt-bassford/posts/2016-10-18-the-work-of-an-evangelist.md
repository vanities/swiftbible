During Bible class Wednesday night, I was asked what the
Biblical definition of the work of the preacher was.  I replied that back when I first started
preaching, I put together a definition by combing through 1 Timothy, 2 Timothy,
and Titus, listing out everything Paul told those two young preachers to do,
and posting the list on the bulletin board in my office.  She asked for the list, and upon reflection, I decided that others
(whether preachers or not) might be interested in the list too.  Certainly, any preacher who does these things
will do well.  Here it is:



**1 Timothy**

* Instruct certain men not to teach strange doctrines, nor to
  pay attention to myths and genealogies (1:3-4).
* Fight the good fight, keeping faith and a good conscience
  (1:18-19).
* Have nothing to do with worldly fables fit only for old
  women (4:7).
* Discipline yourself for the purpose of godliness (4:7).
* Let no one look down on your youthfulness (4:12).
* In speech, conduct, love, faith, and purity, show yourself
  an example of those who believe (4:12).
* Give attention to the public reading of Scripture, to
  exhortation and teaching (4:13).
* Do not neglect the spiritual gift within you (4:14).
* Take pains with these things; be absorbed in them, so that
  your progress will be evident to all (4:15)
* Pay close attention to yourself and your teaching (4:16)
* Persevere in these things (4:16)
* Do not sharply rebuke an older man, but rather appeal to him
  as a father, to the younger men as brothers, the older women as mothers, and
  the younger women as sisters, in all purity (5:1)
* Do not receive an accusation against an elder except on the
  basis of two or three witnesses (5:19)
* Those who continue in sin, rebuke in the presence of all
  (5:20)
* Maintain these principles without bias, doing nothing in a
  spirit of partiality (5:21)
* Do not lay hands upon anyone too hastily and thereby share
  responsibility for the sins of others (5:22)
* Keep yourself free from sin (5:22)
* Flee from [the love of money] (6:11).
* Pursue righteousness, godliness, faith, love, perseverance,
  and gentleness (6:11).
* Fight the good fight of faith (6:12).
* Take hold of the eternal life to which you were called
  (6:12).
* Keep the commandment without stain or reproach (6:14).
* Instruct those who are rich in this present world not to be
  conceited or fix their hope on the uncertainty of riches, but on God (6:17).
* Instruct them to do good, to be rich in good works, to be
  generous and ready to share (6:18).
* Guard what has been entrusted to you, avoiding worldly and
  empty chatter and the opposing arguments of what is falsely called knowledge
  (6:20).



**2 Timothy**

* Kindle afresh the gift of God which is within you (1:6).
* Do not be ashamed of the testimony of our Lord or of me His
  prisoner (1:8).
* Join with me in suffering for the gospel (1:8).
* Retain the standard of sound words which you have heard from
  me (1:13).
* Guard, through the Holy Spirit who dwells in us, the
  treasure which has been entrusted to you (1:14).
* The things which you have heard from me in the presence of
  many witnesses, entrust these to faithful men who will be able to teach others
  also (2:2).
* Suffer hardship with me as a good soldier of Christ Jesus
  (2:3).
* Remember Jesus Christ (2:8).
* Solemnly charge them in the presence of God not to wrangle
  about words (2:14).
* Be diligent to present yourself to God as a workman who does
  not need to be ashamed, accurately handling the word of truth (2:15).
* Avoid worldly and empty chatter (2:16).
* Flee from youthful lusts (2:22).
* Pursue righteousness, faith, love, and peace with those who
  call on the Lord from a pure heart (2:22).
* Refuse foolishness and ignorant speculations (2:23).
* Realize this, that in the last days difficult times will
  come (3:1).
* Continue in the things you have learned and become convinced
  of (3:14).
* Preach the word (4:2).
* Be ready in season and out of season (4:2).
* Reprove, rebuke, exhort, with great patience and instruction
  (4:2).
* Be sober in all things (4:5).
* Endure hardship (4:5).
* Do the work of an evangelist (4:5).
* Fulfill your ministry (4:5).



**Titus**

* Speak the things which are fitting for sound doctrine (2:1).
* Urge the young men to be sensible (2:6).
* In all things show yourself to be an example of good deeds
  (2:7).
* Urge bondslaves to be subject to their own masters in
  everything (2:9).
* These things speak and exhort and reprove with all authority
  (2:15).
* Let no one disregard you (2:15).
* Remind them to be subject to rulers, to authorities, to be
  obedient, to be ready for every good deed, to malign no one, to be peaceable,
  gentle, showing consideration for all men (3:1-2).
* Speak confidently, so that those who have believed God will
  be careful to engage in good deeds (3:8).
* Avoid foolish controversies and genealogies and strife and
  disputes about the Law (3:9).
* Reject a factious man after a first and second warning
  (3:10).
