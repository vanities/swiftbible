Two beams conjoined in simple plan,
Just big enough to hold a man,
A moment's work, both rough and plain,
An instrument of thoughtless pain.

A tree of death, accursed wood,
A travesty of right and good,
Where mockers came in hate and pride
To blaspheme there while Mercy died.

A throne to bear a servant-King,
A vessel for His offering,
The altar where His blood was poured,
The cross of Christ, redeeming Lord.
