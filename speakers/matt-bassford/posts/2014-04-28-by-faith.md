*And without faith it is impossible to please Him, for he who comes to God must believe that He is and that He is a rewarder of those who seek Him.*
--Hebrews 11:6 (NASB)

By faith we come to You,
O faithful Lord,
And patiently pursue
Your great reward.
By faith we offer prayers;
By faith, confess;
By faith, become the heirs
Of righteousness.

By faith we hear You speak
In each command
Like Abraham, we seek
A promised land,
But all we leave behind,
As You foretold,
Shall be repaid in kind
A hundredfold.

By faith we praise Your might
At close of day,
And in the gathering night,
We still obey.
In faith we fall asleep
And trust You then;
The faithful, whom You keep,
Shall wake again.
