(Tune:  "Joyful, Joyful, We Adore Thee")

Heights of faith rise up before us,
Bringing stories to our view:
Mountains of Transfiguration
And the Great Commission too.
Sinai, Carmel, and Golgotha--
All of these have truth to share;
If we learn what they can teach us,
They will help us everywhere!
