All glory to the God of grace,

The Father of salvation,

Who called us all to seek His face

And join His new creation!

O magnify this God on high

And hail again the story

Of grace that shows His glory!

Once dead in trespass and in sin,

We called on Him to save us;

He raised us up to life again

Through Christ, the Son He gave us.

With joy we sing, an offering

To Him whose mercy frees us

And brings us near in Jesus.

No longer strangers to His love,

We bow our knees before Him;

Unworthy to be heard above,

By grace, we yet adore Him.

Eternally, our song shall be

This grace too great to measure,

Our portion and our treasure.
