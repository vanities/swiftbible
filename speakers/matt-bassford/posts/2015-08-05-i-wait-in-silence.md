*My soul waits in
silence for God only;
From Him is my salvation.*

-- Psalm 62:1 (NASB)

I wait in silence for the Lord,

The God who keeps my soul,

Which neither peril nor the sword

Can take from His control.



I wait in silence for the Lord

While strife and fear increase,

But none shall plunder my reward

Nor rob me of my peace.

I wait in silence for the Lord

And trust what He has willed,

For all my loss shall be restored

And all my hope fulfilled.
