*But if I say, "I
will not remember Him*

*Or speak anymore in
His name,"*

*Then in my heart it
becomes like a burning fire*

*Shut up in my bones;*

*And I am weary of
holding it in,*

*And I cannot endure
it.*

-- Jeremiah 20:9 (NASB)

Light Your love within my heart;

Jesus, fill me with its burning;

Breathe upon it at the start;

Fan to life its godly yearning.

From the ashes, wake a flame

That will glorify Your name;

From the winds of life, protect it

Till it grows as You direct it.



Feed Your love within my heart;

Never let it fail nor falter;

Lest it dwindle and depart,

Burn my pride upon its altar.

Evil action and desire--

Cast them all into the fire!

Only my complete surrender

Can sustain its holy splendor.

Show Your love within my heart;

Guide the straying, lost, and frightened;

By the light that it imparts,

May their spirits be enlightened.

May their souls be warmed and filled

By the kindness You have willed,

And this radiance not be ended

Till my life has been expended.
