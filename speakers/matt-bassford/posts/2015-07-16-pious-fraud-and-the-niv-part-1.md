Over the past few days, I’ve seen a few variations of the
following message floating around on Facebook. 
I have retained the, um, idiosyncratic grammar and punctuation of the
original.

*Hello
beloved saints,*

*I’m
sure you know that NIV was published by Zondervan but is now OWNED by Harper
Collins, who also publishes the Satanic Bible and the Joy of Gay Sex.*

*The
NIV has now removed 64,575 words from the Bible including Jehovah, Calvary,
Holy Ghost and omnipotent to name but a few. . .*

*The
NIV has also now removed 45 complete verses. 
Most of us have the Bible on our devices and phones.*

*Try
and find these scriptures in NIV on your computer, phone or device right now if
you are in doubt:*

*Matthew
17:21, 18:11, 23:14; Mark 7:16, 9:44, 9:46; Luke 17:36, 23:17; John 5:4; Acts
8:37*

*.
. . you will not believe your eyes.*

*Refuse
to be blinded by Satan, and do not act like you just don’t care.  Let’s not forget what the Lord Jesus said in
John 10:10 (King James Version)*

There are a lot of true things in this piece.  Yes, it is true that Zondervan publishes the
NIV.  Yes, it is true that Zondervan is
owned by HarperCollins.  I assume it’s
true that some division of HarperCollins publishes *The Satanic Bible* and *The Joy
of Gay Sex*; but I’m not going to look up either book on my own just to
check.  It’s at least reasonable that
HarperCollins would publish such things, though.  Yes, it is true that the NIV does not contain
the words “Jehovah”, “Calvary”, “Holy Ghost”, and “omnipotent”, along with
thousands of other words that appear in the old KJV (a theme begins to emerge
here, I think).  Yes, it is true that
none of the verses above appear in the NIV.

However, the post implies that all of these facts are
related, that they somehow add up to a vast, shadowy conspiracy, and that is
ridiculous.

Let’s start with HarperCollins.  As large as HarperCollins is, it is not a
parent company.  Instead, HarperCollins
itself is owned by News Corp, which is a behemoth multinational newspaper and
publishing corporation.  Like every other
publicly held company, News Corp exists to make money, which it does by
publishing anything that it thinks will turn a profit.

Indeed, News Corp sells Bibles to turn a profit.  If (as the post above implies) News Corp were
in the grip of some cartel of Satanists and gay sex fiends and began to corrupt
the Bibles it published to serve their wicked ends, what would happen to those
profits?  What would happen when the many
thousands of conservative Hebrew and Greek scholars in the US and UK realized
that the NIV was not a good-faith translation of the ancient manuscripts?

Believe me; I have no love of any organization that worships
Mammon as fervently as do large multinational corporations.  However, I cannot imagine that any of them
would do something as unprofitable and quite frankly stupid as that.

Five minutes’ study of my copy of the NIV reveals that its
publishers did not, in fact, do any such thing. 
No, the NIV does not say “Jehovah”. 
Instead, like all modern translations, it says “the LORD”.  No, the NIV does not say “Holy Ghost”.  Instead, like all modern translations, it
says “Holy Spirit”.  For that matter, in
Luke 3:16, the NIV says “straps” instead of the KJV’s “latchet”.  Notice a trend here?

I haven’t gone through and checked, but I would imagine that
every one of those 64,575 words that is different in the NIV than in the KJV is
different because the translators rejected the archaic language of the KJV in
favor of modern equivalents that would be more readily comprehended by the
average reader.  However, the modern
equivalents mean pretty much the same thing as the archaisms of the KJV.  Pretending that these changes somehow distort
the meaning of the text is intellectually dishonest.

Likewise, the presentation of the “missing” verses is
intellectually dishonest.  My copy of the
NIV has a bracketed [21] where Matthew 17:21 would be and a footnote next to
the bracket.  At the bottom of the page,
the footnote reads, “Some manuscripts include here words similar to Mark 9:29”.  In other words, this is not a place where the
forces of evil have decided to mutilate the Bible.  Instead, it represents something much drier,
a scholarly disagreement about manuscript evidence and the contents of the
original text of Matthew.

Back in 1611, the KJV translators translated the Bible using
the best manuscripts available to them at the time.  Since 1611, however, a number of older
manuscripts have been discovered.  In
places, those older manuscripts omit verses that the manuscripts used by the
KJV translators included.  Scholars have
studied those places and concluded that the “extra” verses were later additions
that were not part of the original text.

This isn’t a big deal. 
Nothing we believe is dependent on those later additions.  However, I think that all of us still would
like to have our Bibles conform as closely to the originals as possible.  It is in the pursuit of that goal that the
NIV translators omitted the added verses. 
This is not the work of some sinister cabal that aims to overthrow our
faith.  Instead, it is the best judgment
of a committee of conservative Greek scholars about what the inspired text
actually says.

I’m not an NIV guy.  I
really like the translation 99 percent of the time, but I think that the other
1 percent of the time, its efforts to clarify the meaning of the text go wide
of the mark.  I prefer the
formal-equivalence translations, such as the NASB:  a little harder to understand, but more
precise.

However, the above post is a hatchet job plain and
simple.  It misrepresents what the NIV is
actually doing in order to make it seem like any differences from the KJV are
the result of a dastardly scheme cooked up by the enemies of God.  This results not in enlightenment, but in
paranoia and ignorance.

Next time:  why would
somebody write a post like that?
