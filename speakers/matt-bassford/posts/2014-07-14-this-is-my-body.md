*For I received from
the Lord that which I also delivered to you, that the Lord Jesus in the night
in which He was betrayed took bread; and when He had given thanks, He broke it
and said, "This is My body, which is for you; do this in remembrance of Me."*

--1 Corinthians 11:23-24 (NASB)

“This is My body”, the Savior has said,

Showing His love in the breaking of bread;

Humbly we honor His earnest decree:

“Eat of this bread in remembrance of Me.”



“This is My body,” He also declares,

Claiming His church and the union they share,

Members below, while their Head reigns above,

Knit with a bond of unconquerable love.

“This is my body,” let each of us say,

Showing our love for our brethren today;

We who are many are one in one Head,

Joined in one body by breaking one bread.
