At the moment, I’m fresh out of ideas.  The news is dominated by the election, but I
don’t want to write about the election anymore. 
There are only so many different ways I can say, “Those are two evil
people.”

So.  If you’re sick of
reading about the election, what would you like to read about?  As always, I’m interested in hymns and
worship, Bibles and Bible design, and any topic of a spiritual nature or with a
spiritual dimension.  If you’d like my
take on anything in those areas, ask!  I
make no promises, but it’s very likely that I will write something in response.  Leave your request in the comments below or on Facebook, and I'll think it over.
