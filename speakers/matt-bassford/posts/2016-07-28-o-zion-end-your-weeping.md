O Zion, end your weeping;

Lift up your heart, and sing;

In Bethlehem is sleeping

The Christ, your promised King.

Arriving as a stranger,

Yet yours to celebrate,

This baby in a manger,

Though lowly, shall be great.

With sign and proclamation

His gospel word begins:

The knowledge of salvation,

Forgiveness of your sins.

The sunrise that is dawning

Shall make your warfare cease,

For Christ, the star of morning,

Will guide your feet in peace.

With those whose voices thunder

Around the throne above,

Come magnify in wonder

This miracle of love.

In faith, proclaim the story

To comfort all who mourn

While giving God the glory,

"Rejoice!  A child is born!"
