Buy this Bible if:

* You want a Bible with a unique natural-leather cover.
* You want a translation that serves well both in reading and
  in close study.
* You’re looking for a single-column Bible (and if you’re
  getting a journaling Bible at all, you should get it in single column).
* You have eyes that are at least OK in close work.
* You like a paragraphed, rather than a verse-by-verse,
  layout.
* You prefer decently opaque paper.
* You like line-matching.
* You want lots and lots of room to write (or draw, or. . .)
  in.
* You’re willing to pay for that snazzy leather cover.



Don’t buy this Bible if:

* You want a small Bible.
* You’re determined to have a double-column Bible.
* Your eyes are seriously bad for reading.
* You’re insistent that Bibles should be verse-by-verse.
* You want cross-references, ‘cause this one has none.
* You want to go cheap (although the hardback and poly
  editions of this Bible aren’t terribly expensive).
