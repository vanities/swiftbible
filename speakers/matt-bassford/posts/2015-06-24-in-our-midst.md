In our midst, the Lord is present
When we dwell in unity,
And the sight is good and pleasant,
For His work is plain to see.
Like the dew of Hermon falling
Is His wisdom from above
As it calls us to our calling:
Imitating Christ in love.

In our midst, may truth be spoken
With humility and grace;
May the weakest not be broken;
May the stranger find his place.
May our body stay united
Though her strength and cares increase,
That the lost may be invited
Into mercy, love, and peace.

In our midst, may love be fervent;
May our hope and faith abound;
May each saint become a servant
And the mind of Christ be found.
May we grow in these together
By the working of each part,
Joined in fellowship forever,
One in spirit, one in heart.
