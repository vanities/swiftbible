Savior, You will not forsake me;
For Your handiwork, You yearn;
Though I sleep, Your voice will wake me
In the hour of Your return.

Chorus:
I will answer!  I will answer!
Though the night of death may fall;
On the day of resurrection,
I will answer when You call.

This my faith, beyond despising,
This my hope, beyond all doubt:
As You rose, I know my rising;
I will hear You when You shout.

(Chorus)

With my eyes I will behold You,
And no other will I see;
With the glories that enfold You,
I will praise Your victory.

(Chorus)
