*For it was fitting for
us to have such a high priest, holy, innocent, undefiled, separated from
sinners and exalted above the heavens.*

-- Hebrews 7:26 (NASB)

O Christ, divine and sinless,

How greatly I have sinned!

I run, condemned and friendless,

To You, the sinners’ Friend.

That name, which You were given

In mockery and shame,

Is now my path to heaven,

The only hope I claim.



In poverty of spirit,

I kneel to You and plead,

Although I cannot merit

The mercy that I need.

But through Your pardon, written

In blood from hands and side,

My crime may be forgotten,

My soul, be justified.

O Lord, extend Your favor;

Enfold me in Your grace:

My breath from You, O Savior

Who perished in my place;

Then life and death together

Will find me undismayed,

My sin erased forever,

My debt already paid.
