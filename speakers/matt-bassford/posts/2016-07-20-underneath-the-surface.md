(Tune:  "Who Will Follow Jesus?")

Underneath the surface,

Deep in you and me,

Many things are hidden

No one else can see,

But the love of Jesus

Knows the hidden part;

With His perfect wisdom,



He can search the heart.

The above is our VBS for this year.  If you're in the area, come.  If you're not in the area, you can still come.  For that matter, there will be an adult class in the auditorium during every session, so you don't even have to have a kid to come.  Basically, show up.  It'll be good.
