God will not forsake His own

While He reigns upon His throne.

By His mercy and His might,

He will bring His plans to light.

God will not forsake His own,

Though they grieve, oppressed and lone.

In compassion through despair,

Still His grace is present there.

God will not forsake His own

On the path He has foreknown.

At its ending, they will see

Comfort, joy, and victory.
