God is waiting for our prayer;

Let us bring Him our petition:

Sweet as incense in the air,

Rich with worship and contrition.

Boldly, boldly, let us cry,

Swift to seek and glorify!



God is waiting for our woe;

Let us seek Him in our weeping;

When our sorrows overflow,

May we give them to His keeping.

Boldly, boldly let us cry

To our Comforter nearby!

God is waiting for our need;

Let us pray in each endeavor;

Who can help us when we plead

Like the King who reigns forever?

Boldly, boldly let us cry

To our Answer from on high!
