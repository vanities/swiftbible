Buy this Bible if:

* You’re looking for a small Bible you can fit in your purse,
  backpack, or glove box.
* You want a ruggedly constructed Bible that will stand up to
  a pounding.
* You’re interested in a translation that is excellent for
  close study.
* You prefer single-column Bibles.
* You like Bibles that put the text in paragraphs.
* You want a relatively inexpensive beater Bible.

Don’t buy this Bible if:

* You want your Bible covers made from the skins of dead
  goats, or at least dead pigs.
* You want a translation that’s very easy to read, even if it
  loses something in precision.
* You prefer more traditional double-column Bibles.
* You need a large-print Bible.
* You insist on Bibles with verse-by-verse format.
* You require a Bible with cross-references.
* You want a Bible with heavy-for-a-Bible, opaque paper.
* You want your Bible to have line-matching.
* You are looking for a Bible with wide margins suitable for
  writing in.
