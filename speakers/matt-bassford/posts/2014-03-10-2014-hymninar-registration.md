Greetings, all!

I am delighted to announce the OFFICIAL beginning of
registration for the 2014 Hymninar!  “What
IS a Hymninar?” you might ask.  Good
question!  The Hymninar is a weeklong
hymn-writing workshop that runs from August 4-8 in Columbia, MO.  All levels of experience will be represented
therein, from Christians who have been writing hymns for decades to (we hope!)
those who have never so much as made an attempt at it before.   If you
have an interest in the subject and you would enjoy (or at least tolerate)
spending a week among the godly, moderately weird, and occasionally funny, this
is the workshop for you.

Structurally, the first few days of the workshop will be
employed in studying *A Good Hymn*, an
aptly named workbook that distills the fundamental
principles of hymnody from a selection of good and, um, not-so-good hymns.  Students will then spend the remainder of the
time working on a single four-line verse, which they will endeavor to raise to
a publishable level of quality.  This is
easier to describe than it is to do.



Repeat attendees will also have the opportunity to submit
polished hymns to review by the group. 
These review sessions bear no resemblance to the spectacle of an elk
being dragged down and torn apart by starving wolves.  None whatsoever.

So. . . um. . . boring stuff.  The direct cost of this will run about
$85ish.  This includes the cost of the class
itself and parking.  Because the brethren
in Columbia are really, really nice, they offer attendees the opportunity to
stay in their homes throughout the week. 
If that doesn’t suit, you may lodge in any of the area hotels at your own
expense.    Attendees are also generally responsible for
scaring up their own lunches and dinners, though occasional generosity from the
brethren may show up here too.

If you have found this description insufficiently
discouraging and want to attend, you can register by getting me your name and
contact info.  Private-message me
(Matthew William Bassford) on Facebook, e-mail me at mandlbassford@gmail.com, or
even call me at (815)931-1740.  If you do
this and don’t hear back from me in a week or two, try again.  I have the administrative aptitude of a
kumquat and may well benefit from the reminder.

Once you’re registered, in a few months, you’ll get a letter that will explain what I’ve said in greater detail and loftier language.  Until then, just send me your info, and I
(or, more accurately, my wife, who has enough administrative aptitude for both
of us) will put you on the list.  Last day for registration is July 7th, 2014.
