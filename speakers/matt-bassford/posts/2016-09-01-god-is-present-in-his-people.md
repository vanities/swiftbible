God is present in His people,

Not in works of wood and stone,

For the temple of the spirit

Is the house He makes His own.

God is here, and He is working!

Do not doubt, nor wonder how;

Through the hands upraised to serve Him,

He is working even now.

God is present in His people,

Present, though the flesh is weak,

In the hope of grace and heaven,

In the righteousness they seek.

Though the body now is groaning,

Wracked with wars that never cease,

Still the mind set on the spirit

Is forever life and peace.

God is present in His people,

In forgiveness of their sin,

In their love and in their service,

In the triumph they shall win.

May we all invite His presence,

Laying hold of His reward,

For the hearts where He is present

Shall be present with the Lord.
