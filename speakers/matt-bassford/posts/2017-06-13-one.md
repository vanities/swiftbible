We share this union in the bond of peace:
One body, where divisiveness must cease,
One Spirit who indwells and leads us all,
One hope belonging to our common call.

One Lord, the Christ whose coming we confess,
One faith, accounted as our righteousness,
One birth that purifies us in the Son,
One God and Father over everyone.
