*"By this all men will know that you are
My disciples, if you have love for one another."*

-- John 13:35

O Lord, our life, our
righteousness,

Who leads us to the things above,

We seek the heart that You
possess;

We seek the perfect bond of love.



We seek the love that will
forgive

Despite the ravages of sin,

That helps the dying sinner live

To share Your holiness again.

We seek the love that helps the
weak,

Sustains the old, and guides the
youth,

The love that knows just how to
speak,

But never fears to speak the
truth.

Thus may our love for others
grow:

The love that bears, believes,
endures,

That by our love, all men will
know

That we are followers of Yours.
