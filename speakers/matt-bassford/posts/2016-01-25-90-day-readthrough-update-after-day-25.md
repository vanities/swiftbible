This morning's observation from the 90-day Bible readthrough: my goodness, but reading 16 chapters of 1 and 2 Kings at a time is a long reading! I may go through and re-order that (just like I'm going to put Deuteronomy 23 at the end of a reading rather than the beginning!). However, it was kind of cool to go through the whole rise and fall of the house of Ahab in a sitting.

Also, I'm beginning to think that Bible design is more important with a reading Bible than with a pulpit Bible. Even when I'm preaching for 45 minutes, I simply don't spend that much time engaged with the Bible in my hand. I'm primarily focused on connecting with the congregation, not the Book!

However, if I'm reading 16 *long* chapters of Israelite history, even though I'm a fast reader, that takes me a while to get through. That much time will magnify any flaws in layout and construction. It is far easier to plow through the reading with a top-notch layout and excellent book block in the Caxton than it would be in a $15 Wal-Mart special!
