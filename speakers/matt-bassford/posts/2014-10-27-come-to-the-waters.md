*"Ho! Every one who thirsts, come to the waters;*

*And you who have no money come, buy and eat.*

*Come, buy wine and milk*

*Without money and without cost.*

-- Isaiah 55:1 (NASB)

Come to the waters, you who thirst

For pardon from your sin;

From Christ the Rock a flood has burst

To quench the guilt within.



Come to the waters, you who flee

The evils of your past;

Here God will give you victory

And freedom to the last.

Come to the waters, you who mourn;

Be buried with the Lord;

Then rise, forgiven and reborn,

An heir to His reward.
