I generated this list using three criteria:

1.       DOCTRINAL DEPTH.  Does the hymn have something spiritually
meaningful to say?  Is its meaning
profound or superficial?

2.      CONGREGATIONAL SUITABILITY.  Is the hymn simple enough that an average
a-cappella congregation (80 in attendance on Sunday morning) can sing it easily?

3.      CONGREGATIONAL APPEAL.  Do worshipers enjoy singing this hymn?  Is it widely used?



Additionally, I did not consider (and will not
include) any hymn written by me or by anyone I know to avoid introducing
bias.

There are many worthy hymns that did not make the
Top-50 cut.  If you think an omitted hymn
should be on the list, leave a comment identifying both it and the hymn that
you think it should replace.  You may
change my mind!
