Hi, friends! It's been just over 6 months since Matt went to meet Jesus and rest in Abraham's bosom. I've debated what to do with his page. What I've decided on for now is that it will be a kind of landing page for Matt and his work. All of the blog posts on here will stay, indefinitely.

In addition to the blog posts, though, I'll also use this to update people on Matt's work and writing as it becomes available. He left a wealth of writing that hasn't ever been formally published, so I intend to work through that bit by bit and preserve what I can for future generations.

In the meantime, I'm including links to Matt's obituary, as well as a couple of organizations that Matt worked with in case you're looking for a way to financially honor him. I am so grateful to all of you for your love for us and for Matt!

In love,

Lauren (and the kids)
