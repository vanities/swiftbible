*Oh, give ear, Shepherd
of Israel,*

*You who lead Joseph
like a flock;*

*You who are enthroned
above the cherubim, shine forth!*

-- Psalms 80:1 (NASB)

Shepherd of Israel,

Jesus, our Savior,

Comfort Your weary flock;

Bless us forever.

Straighten our crooked path;

Level its mountains,

Guide through the wilderness;

Fill it with fountains.



Shepherd of Israel,

Kind to attend us,

Stay thru the circling night;

Calm and defend us.

Call to Your sheep by name;

Watch us, unsleeping.

Teach us to give our souls

Into Your keeping.

Shepherd of Israel,

Wise and all-knowing,

Train us to know the Way

Where we are going.

Help us walk patiently

Where You ascended,

Till we may rest with You

Where fear has ended.
