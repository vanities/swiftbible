*“The secret things
belong to the Lord our God, but
the things revealed belong to us and to our sons forever, that we may observe
all the words of this law.”*

-- Deuteronomy 29:29 (NASB)



The secret things belong to You,

O God who dwells in light,

And all that we may think or do

Lies open to Your sight.

The secret things belong to You,

Through all that we intend;

The course our moments will pursue,

You know from start to end.

The secret things belong to You;

They fill Your mind alone,

And if You hide them from our view,

We cannot make them known.

The secret things belong to You,

But You make clear Your word,

That we and that our children too

May do as we have heard.
