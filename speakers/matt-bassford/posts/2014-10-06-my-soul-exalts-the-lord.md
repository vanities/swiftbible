*And Mary said: “My
soul exalts the Lord, And my spirit has rejoiced in God my Savior.”*

-- Luke 1:46-47 (NASB)

My soul exalts the Lord;

To God, I lift my voice;

In His salvation, I will sing,

And in His hope, rejoice.

In Him, I find my strength

Through troubles dark and grim,

For none is holy like the Lord,

Nor any rock like Him.



My soul exalts the Lord

Because the Lord is great,

For He has had regard for me,

And for my humble state.

He fills the poor with bread,

But bids the rich depart;

He lifts the needy from the dust,

But shames the proud of heart.

My soul exalts the Lord,

Who reigns in heaven yet;

The pillars of the earth are His

On which the world is set.

The Lord shall be my help,

As He has sworn before,

And give to all who fear His name

His mercy evermore.
