Buy this Bible (one of these Bibles) if:

* You’re fine with a mid-size reading Bible.
* You want a high-premium, edge-lined Bible with a goatskin
  cover and art gilding.
* You’re looking for a translation that’s easy to read and
  understand but doesn’t have to be very precise.
* You can handle mid-size font if it’s printed clearly.
* You want a paragraphed Bible.
* You want cross-references (though from my perspective, the
  primary purpose of the cross-references is to make the text column narrower).
* You’re OK with less-than-opaque paper, provided the setting
  is line-matched.
* You don’t want to write in the margin.
* You’re willing to pay between $100 and $200 for a Bible.
* Buy a Select if you want a more subdued cover.
* Buy a Caxton if you want a slightly busier cover (still not
  busy; this is a Bible, after all), a calfskin liner, and better ribbons.

Don’t buy this Bible if:

* You want a small carry Bible.
* You want a translation that excels at close study.
* You need a large-print Bible.
* You insist on a verse-by-verse format.
* Opaque paper is a must.
* You want a Bible you can write a lot in.
* You think nearly $200 is smartphone money, not Bible money.
