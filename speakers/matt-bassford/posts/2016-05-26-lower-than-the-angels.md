Lower than the angels

For a little while,

Christ, the Word incarnate,

Silent at His trial.

See the Lamb unblemished,

Sun of righteousness,

Sentenced for the kingship

That He dares confess.

Lower than the angels

For a little while;

All of His disciples,

Fled in their denial.

Now the hope of Israel,

Heir to David’s throne,

Stumbles to Golgotha,

Bleeding and alone.

Lower than the angels

For a little while;

Hear the priests and elders

Slander and revile:

“Save Yourself, Messiah,

“And we will believe!”

While the heavens darken

And the angels grieve.

Lower than the angels

For a little while,

Jesus, crowned with glory

Death could not defile.

Perfect through His suffering,

He will intercede,

Taught to give us mercy

In our time of need.
