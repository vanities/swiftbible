This is the autograph. 
I found it about a month ago, after years of searching, while going
through some old papers of mine that my dad had saved for me.  I wrote this exactly 17 years ago today.  When I consider who I was and all that has
happened since, I can only echo Ezra's judgment in Ezra 9:8, that “now for a brief
moment grace has been shown from the Lord our God.”  Some things are beyond me.  Some things are gifts.  This will always be one of them.
