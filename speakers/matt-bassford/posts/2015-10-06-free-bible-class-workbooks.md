Over the weekend, I spent some time with a preacher friend of mine.  As we were talking, I realized that even though we'd known each other for several years, he didn't know that I'd written a whole mess of freely available Bible-class workbooks.  I generally write three workbooks in the course of a year, and sometime in the next year, our webmaster uploads them to the Joliet church's website.

They're available under a Creative Commons license that allows you to download and reproduce them freely, without asking permission, so long as you don't alter them, sell them, or remove my name from them.  I won't say they're the most insightful Bible-class workbooks ever written, but there are dozens of them, and they're free.  You can download them all at <http://jolietchurch.com/workbooks.html>.
