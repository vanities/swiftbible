O Son of God on high,
So near in sympathy,
You hear my mourning when I cry
And freely mourn with me.

When I must grieve below,
You also grieve above;
You share my heavy hours of woe
And lighten each with love.

The sorrow I endure
Is sorrow You have known;
Because You wept, I may be sure
I'll never weep alone.

"I'll Never Weep Alone", along with a number of other fine hymns, recently appeared on Sing to the Lord's album *The Bible Stands*, which you can find for sale [here.](http://www.cdbaby.com/cd/sotc)  My copy of the album hasn't shown up yet, but the preview sounds lovely!
