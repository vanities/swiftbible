*Then Jacob awoke from his sleep and said, "Surely the LORD is in this place, and I did not know it."* Genesis 28:16 (NASB)

Surely God is in this place,

Though we cannot see His face;

When His children gather here,

He has promised to be near.

Earth and sky cannot contain

All the splendors of His reign,

Yet He joins us from above:

Rich in mercy, great in love.



Surely God is in this place

In the fullness of His grace.

By His bidding, let us bring

All our sorrows to our King.

He is sure to save and bless

In His constant faithfulness;

When He hears us in our plight,

What can stand against His might?

Surely God is in this place,

Unconstrained by time or space;

When His chosen people meet,

In their midst, He takes His seat.

Everywhere the righteous flee,

There the Lord
shall always be,

Here, yet hidden from our sight

Till we dwell with Him in light.
