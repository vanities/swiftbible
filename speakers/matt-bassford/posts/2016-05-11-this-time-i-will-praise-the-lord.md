God will bless
the brokenhearted,

And His
goodness I will see;

Though my glory
has departed,

He is near to
comfort me.

Standing here
amid the rubble

Where my
earthly hopes had soared,

I will turn to
Him in trouble;

This time, I
will praise the Lord.

Now I see my
former blindness;

Now I see my
plans were vain,

Yet in God is
lovingkindness,

And His mercies
will remain.

With His grace,
He will anoint me;

He will grant a
sure reward;

He will never
disappoint me;

This time, I
will praise the Lord.

For the gifts
that He has given,

For the gifts
He will not give,

I will praise
the God of heaven;

I will share in
Him, and live.

In the joy of
present blessing,

In the hope of
loss restored,

By my faith, I
come confessing;
This time, I
will praise the Lord.
