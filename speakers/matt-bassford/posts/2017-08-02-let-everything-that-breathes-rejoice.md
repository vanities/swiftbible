Let everything that breathes rejoice

In God, who made each one;

Let form and beauty, call and voice

Declare what He has done.

Let fish that teem within the sea,

Let birds that soar above

Pay tribute to His majesty

And magnify His love.

Let all that moves upon the earth

In myriad shapes and ways

Proclaim their Maker from their birth

And till their death give praise.

With them, may we accept our place

And join His praise by choice;

Lift up your hearts, o heirs of grace!

With all that breathes, rejoice!
