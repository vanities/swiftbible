*Serve the LORD with
gladness;*

*Come before Him with
joyful singing.*

--Psalms 100:2 (NASB)

Come before the Lord with gladness,

You who walk in all His ways;

Cast aside your fear and sadness;

Bring the sacrifice of praise.

Magnify His strength and splendor;

Tell the marvels He has done;

Come with songs of hope and wonder;

Come exalt the Holy One.



Praise the Lord, the God of heaven,

Ruler over time and space;

Every gift and good thing given,

We have tasted by His grace.

Every blessing in creation,

Every promised joy above,

Patience, mercy, and salvation--

All, the bounty of His love.

Thankful heart cannot deny Him,

God, the Holy and Adored;

Surely we will glorify Him!

Surely we will bless the Lord!

Come, our King, and leave us never;

On our praises, take Your throne;

Dwell with us, our God forever,

We, a people for Your own.
