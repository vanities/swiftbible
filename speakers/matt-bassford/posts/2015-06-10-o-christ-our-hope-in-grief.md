O Christ, our hope in grief,
Our comforter in pain,
We seek You for relief
From wounds that long remain.
No longer can our spirits bear
Their weight of anguish and despair.

From slavery to sin,
From depths of hidden shame,
We cry to You again
For refuge in Your name.
Without Your grace and sympathy,
What help or healing can there be?

We must rely on You,
Both powerful and pure,
For mercy to renew,
For courage to endure
Until the dawning of the day
When You will wipe each tear away.
