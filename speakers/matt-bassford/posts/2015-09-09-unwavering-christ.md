Unwavering Christ, forever the same,
In awe and in joy, I sing to Your name.
O sun of my heart, so constant above,
You brighten and warm each day with Your love.

Unwavering Christ, so kind to the weak,
Your love has poured out the grace that I seek.
In me, it springs up in perfect supply,
A river of life that never runs dry.

Unwavering Christ, the faithful and true,
To night and beyond, my hope is in You.
On glimpsing Your face, forever I'll shine,
The glory all Yours, the victory mine!
