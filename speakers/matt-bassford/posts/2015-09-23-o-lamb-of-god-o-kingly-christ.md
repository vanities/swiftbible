*The next day he saw
Jesus coming to him and said, “Behold, the Lamb of God who takes away the sin
of the world!”*

-- John 1:29 (NASB)

O Lamb of God, O kingly Christ,

Hear now our spirits’ sacrifice:

As we have breath, we praise You yet,

Stirred by the greatness of our debt.



Since You have perished in our place,

Our lives have been restored by grace;

For all You’ve done, what can we do

But offer song and life to You?

If we should sleep beneath the ground,

Still will Your righteousness resound,

For when the dead in Christ are raised,

Even our dust shall sing Your praise.
