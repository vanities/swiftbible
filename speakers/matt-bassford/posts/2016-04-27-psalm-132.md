Several months ago, I was invited to work on the *Timeless* project.  The goal of this project is to produce a new psalter, one which retains the themes of the original Psalms while making applications relevant to Christians.  Though the *Timeless* editors haven't said so, I envision the results looking something like Isaac Watts' "The Lord My Shepherd Is".  The hymn below is my first take on Psalm 132.


Remember us, O God of grace;

O Bull of Jacob, hear us!

We long to be Your dwelling place,

So bring Your presence near us.

Arise in strength, O God, to bless

The church You have appointed;

Your priests are dressed in righteousness;

Return to Your anointed.

To David was Your mercy shown,

By oath and by endeavor;

You set his offspring on his throne:

The Christ, who reigns forever.

By Him, You call us children too;

By Him, Your grace can reach us,

As long as we are sure to do

The words of truth You teach us.

Your love has chosen us to be

Your holy habitation;

Sustain our souls with Your decree,

And clothe us with salvation.

Your faithful ones will surely sing

And magnify Your story,

For You have given us a King
Whose crown You light with glory.
