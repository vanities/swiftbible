Christ, only Christ is my hope of salvation;

Christ, only Christ can restore me again.

Who is my guard against snare and temptation?

Christ, only Christ will protect me from sin.

Christ, only Christ is my comfort in sorrow;

Christ, only Christ is my healer and guide.

Sure through the dangers and doubts of tomorrow,

Christ, only Christ will remain by my side.

Christ, only Christ will conduct me to glory;

Christ, only Christ can prevail in that strife.

Even when death and its fears lie before me,

Christ, only Christ is the promise of life.
