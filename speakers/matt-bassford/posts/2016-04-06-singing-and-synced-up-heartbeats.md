OK.  [This is just cool.](http://www.npr.org/sections/health-shots/2013/07/09/200390454/when-choirs-sing-many-hearts-beat-as-one?)  To summarize, a study found that when a choir
sings together, the heart rates of the choir members quickly sync up.  Apparently, when Luke says in Acts 4:32 that,
“the full number of those who believed were of one heart. . .”, he isn’t
speaking metaphorically!  Not entirely, at least.  The more I
learn, the more I am amazed by God’s wisdom in instructing His people to sing.  Our song worship unites us in body, in mind,
and in spirit.  And, apparently, in
heart.
