*“But I say to you,
love your enemies and pray for those who persecute you, so that you may be sons
of your Father who is in heaven; for He causes His sun to rise on the evil and
the good, and sends rain on the righteous and the unrighteous.”*

-- Matthew 5:44-45 (NASB)

O Father, merciful and kind,

You make Your sun to rise;

And daily, as Your love designed,

It cheers the gloomy skies.

It warms the evil and the good,

The sinner and the saint;

On all the human brotherhood,

You shine without restraint.



In providence, You send the rain,

A bounty from Your hand;

It falls upon the thirsty plain

And nourishes the land.

Your mercy satisfies the just,

But feeds the wicked too,

For all the children of the dust

Sustain their life in You.

You call us not to love our own,

For sinners do the same,

Nor share Your gifts with those alone

Who call upon Your name.

“But love your enemies,” You say,

“That you may be My sons,”

For by our mercy, we display

Your love for everyone.
