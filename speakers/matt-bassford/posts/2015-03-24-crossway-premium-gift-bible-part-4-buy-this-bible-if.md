Buy this Bible if:

* You’re looking for a smallish Bible but don’t need a true
  compact.
* You’re fine with a polyurethane cover, which is probably the
  best combination of high durability and low cost out there.
* You want a generally word-for-word translation that isn’t as
  extreme as the NASB.
* You like double-column Bibles.
* You can read smallish, normal-for-a-Bible, print.
* You prefer Bible text that is set in normal English
  paragraphs.
* You don’t need any cross-references beyond citations for
  Old-Testament passages quoted in the New Testament, parallel passages in the
  Synoptic gospels, and perhaps a few others of similar importance.
* You can handle paper with a fair amount of see-through.
* You don’t require a line-matched Bible.
* You aren’t intending to write much, if at all, in the
  margins.
* You’re looking for a Bible with decent quality at a low
  price.



Don’t buy this Bible if:

* You either need a smaller Bible or prefer larger ones.
* You want a Bible with a high-quality, premium cover.  This ain’t it.
* You want a translation that has been smoothed out for easy
  reading.  The ESV is better than the NASB
  in this regard, but it’s still more difficult to follow than the NLT, for
  instance.
* Your eyes aren’t very good at close work, and you need a
  Bible with large or better print.
* You want every verse in your Bible to have its own
  paragraph.
* You want a Bible with a full suite of tens of thousands of
  cross-references.
* You require opaque paper without a lot of show-through from
  the other side.
* You insist that your Bibles be line-matched.
* You want to write a large number of notes in the margin of
  your Bible.
* You aren’t particularly concerned with how much your Bible
  is going to cost.  Crossway produces much
  nicer versions of this setting, all the way up to the top-of-the-line Heirloom
  Thinline, which in goatskin retails for $230. 
  Basically, there’s sure to be a version of the Thinline at your price
  point.  It’s one of Crossway’s
  best-selling settings.
