*In my distress I called upon the LORD,*
*And cried to my God for help;*
*He heard my voice out of His temple,*
*And my cry for help before Him came into His ears.*
-- Psalm 18:6 (NASB)

I love the Lord, in whom I hide,
Because He heard me when I cried;
I called in my distress and fear,
And my petition reached His ear.


The mountains trembled at His frown;
He bowed the heavens and came down;
Upon the wings of wind, He sped,
And clouds of darkness wreathed His head.

With thunderbolts and coals of fire,
He smote the wicked in His ire;
From evil and calamity,
The Lord my stay delivered me.

The words and ways of God are tried;
I find a refuge by His side;
Who is a rock amid the fight
Like God, who girds my soul with might?
