Buy this Bible if:

* You want a handy, easy-to-carry Bible.
* You want a wide-margin Bible that offers you a stable
  note-taking surface.
* You’re at least fine with a conservative translation
  descended from the King James Version
* You prefer single-column Bibles.
* You like your Bibles paragraphed.
* You require comparatively thick, opaque paper that will
  limit ghosting and bleed-through.
* You appreciate line-matching in a Bible.
* You want great big margins to write in (this is *the* reason to buy this Bible)
* You want one of the more inexpensive wide-margin Bibles on
  the market.

Don’t buy this Bible if:

* You want your Bible cover made out of goatskin, or even
  simply genuine leather.
* You want a freer, easier-to-read translation.
* You demand double-column Bibles.
* You don’t handle small print well.
* You prefer verse-by-verse Bibles.
* You require cross-references (though you can always write
  these in).
