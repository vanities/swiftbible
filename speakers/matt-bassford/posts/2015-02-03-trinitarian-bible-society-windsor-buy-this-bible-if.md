**Buy This Bible If:**

* You prefer or at least tolerate the KJV.
* You like double-column layouts better than single-column
  layout.
* Your eyes aren’t absolutely miserable at close work.
* It’s important to you to locate verses quickly and easily
  with a verse-by-verse setting.
* You like a smaller Bible for ease of carrying.
* You don’t particularly need cross-references.
* You like the idea of getting a premium-quality Bible at a
  mass-market price.

**Don’t Buy This Bible
If:**

* As far as you’re concerned, the KJV might as well be written
  in Klingon.
* You cannot abide double-column layouts (though note that the
  only standard-text single-column KJV of which I’m aware is the pricey Cambridge
  Clarion).
* You prefer that the poetry of the Old Testament be set in
  verse.
* You need a super-duper large-print Bible.
* You favor the flow of a paragraphed Bible (though again, the
  KJV Clarion may be your only hope here).
* You rely on the “Bible extras” that come with larger
  Bibles—cross-references, wide margins, and so on.
* You’re on either end of the Bible-quality spectrum:  Either you will not use a Bible unless it
  comes with goatskin and art gilt, or you have so little money that the best you
  can afford is a glued KJV at Wal-Mart. 
  Even in the latter case, though, this Bible will save you money in the
  long run.  It’s well worth making an
  investment in.
