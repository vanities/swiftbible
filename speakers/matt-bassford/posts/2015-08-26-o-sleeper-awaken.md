O sleeper, awaken,

And rise from the dead,

No longer forsaken

But joyful instead,

For over your sorrows

The Savior will shine

To bless your tomorrows

With mercy divine.

O sleeper, awaken,

No more to be found

In error, mistaken,

In slavery, bound,

But seek your salvation

And look on His face:

The full revelation

Of truth and of grace.

O sleeper, awaken

And rise from the dead,

Not fearing nor shaken

In Jesus your Head.

In Him, you shall never

Be lost to the night,

But prosper forever

In peace and delight.
