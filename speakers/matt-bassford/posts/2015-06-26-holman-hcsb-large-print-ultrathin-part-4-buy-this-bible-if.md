Buy this Bible if:

* You want a fairly large Bible that is still light in the
  hand.
* Having a soft, thick cover is important to you.
* You want a translation that is easy to read.
* You prefer double-column Bibles.
* Reading gives your eyes at most moderate trouble.
* You like Bibles in which the text is set in paragraphs.
* You want a full cross-reference suite.
* Paper opacity doesn’t concern you.
* You like line-matched Bibles.
* You don’t intend to write in the margins.
* You’re willing to pay extra for a sumptuous cover.

Don’t buy this Bible if:

* You need a small, compact Bible to fit in a purse or glove
  box.
* You are indifferent to nice covers.
* You want a translation that is well suited for close study.
* You require a single-column Bible.
* Your eyes struggle with reading and other close work.
* You prefer Bibles in which each verse is set in its own
  paragraph.
* You demand opaque paper (REALLY don’t buy this Bible,
  then!).
* You want a Bible you can write in.
* You don’t want to buy a Bible that retails for $120.  Note, though, that much cheaper versions of
  this same Bible are readily available.
