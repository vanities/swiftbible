We are the Lord’s, no longer our own:

Striving in faith to serve Him alone,

Seeking the crown which He will award,

So if we live, we live for the Lord.

Fixed upon Him whose promise is plain,

Year after year, our hope will remain,

Sure despite age or sickness or sword,

For if we die, we die for the Lord.

Thus we press on with prayer and with praise,

Ready to face the trials of our days;

They will abound with certain rewards;

Living or dead, still we are the Lord’s.
