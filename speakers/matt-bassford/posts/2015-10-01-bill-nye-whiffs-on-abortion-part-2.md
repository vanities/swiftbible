For those of you who missed the first installment, I’m talking about the recent Bill Nye pro-choice video, which you can find [here.](http://www.cnet.com/news/bill-nye-brings-science-to-bear-on-the-abortion-debate/)

Toward the end of Part 1, I noted that Nye described the
pro-life movers and shakers as “men of European descent”.  The full quotation here is, “You cannot help
but notice—I’m not the first guy to observe this—that you have a lot of men of
European descent passing extreme laws based on ignorance.”

In other words, pro-lifers are not only stupid.  They’re also sexist and racist.  We’ve dealt with stupid already.  Let’s talk about sexist and racist.



Actually, let’s talk about one racist in particular.  Let’s talk about Margaret Sanger, the revered
founder of Planned Parenthood.  Even
though Sanger has gained the modern reputation of being the Mother Theresa of
birth control, her actual words and writings reveal her to be a nasty-bad
eugenicist with more than a tinge of racism. 
Consider the following quotation from a 1921 Sanger article entitled
“The Eugenic Value of Birth Control Propaganda”:

Today Eugenics is suggested by the
most diverse minds as the most adequate and thorough avenue to the solution of
racial, political and social problems. The most intransigent and daring
teachers and scientists have lent their support to this great biological
interpretation of the human race. The war has emphasized its necessity.

Also:

In this matter, the example of the inferior classes, the fertility of the
feeble-minded, the mentally defective, the poverty-stricken classes, should not
be held up for emulation to the mentally and physically fit though less fertile
parents of the educated and well-to-do classes. On the contrary, the most
urgent problem today is how to limit and discourage the over-fertility of the
mentally and physically defective.

Similarly, in her 1922 book *The Pivot of Civilization*, Sanger wrote:

Here is a typical case showing the astonishing ability to “increase and
multiply,” organically bound up with delinquency and defect of various
types:  “The parents of a feeble-minded
girl, twenty years of age, who was committed to the Kansas State Industrial
Farm on a vagrancy charge, lived in a thickly populated Negro district which
was reported by the police to be the headquarters for the criminal element of
the surrounding State....  The mother
married at fourteen, and her first child was born at fifteen. In rapid
succession she gave birth to sixteen live-born children and had one
miscarriage.”

It’s terrible how those feeble-minded Negroes have so many
children, isn’t it?

Not surprisingly, this sort of rhetoric was well-received by
the most racist elements of American society at the time.  In her autobiography, Sanger recounts an
anecdote about a 1926 speech she gave to the women’s branch of the New Jersey
Ku Klux Klan.  They so appreciated what
she had to say that she received “a dozen invitations to speak to similar
groups.”

Today, Planned Parenthood invests a lot of effort in
sweeping the sordid history of its founder under the rug, but her legacy
continues.  According to a 2008 report by
the Guttmacher Institute, which is joined at the hip to Planned Parenthood,
African-American women receive abortions at a rate five times that of white
women.  Note that Sanger also coined the phrase
“birth control”.  One wonders who is
doing the controlling.

Of course, we don’t want to forget about “sexist”.  How empowering abortion is for women!  Especially in China.  Back in 1980, the Chinese government
instituted a policy that limited most families to only one child.  However, Chinese society is strongly
patriarchal.  Among other things, Chinese
couples are expected to care for the aging parents of the husband, not the
wife.  Once ultrasound technology
emerged, expectant mothers were able to select for the desired son by aborting
undesired daughters.

At birth, the normal human sex ratio is something like 105
boys for every 100 girls.  In China in
2010, the ratio had risen to 119 boys for every 100 girls.  Abortion has empowered something like 10
percent of all Chinese girls right out of existence!

Let’s throw in another one that Nye might have added if he’d
thought of it:  ableism.  At 15-18 weeks of gestational age, most women
in developed countries take the “triple test” to determine whether their fetus
is at risk for trisomy 21 (which leads to Down syndrome) and a number of
other genetic defects.

When first I encountered the triple test, it baffled
me.  It’s not like you can undo trisomy
21.  What’s the point?  Eventually, I realized what was going
on.  The triple test offers mothers the
opportunity to opt out from parenting children with genetic defects by aborting
them.  In the US, something like
two-thirds of fetuses diagnosed with Down syndrome are indeed aborted.

I don’t know why this one bothers me particularly, but it
does.  Sure; a baby born with Down syndrome probably isn’t going to Harvard one day, but all of the people with
Down syndrome whom I’ve known have been gentle, amiable souls who would never hurt
anyone.  They deserve decency and kindness,
not erasure.

There’s a trend that runs through all three of these
examples.  In any society, it’s not the
future top dogs who get aborted.  It’s
not those who belong to privileged classes. 
Instead, it’s those whom society deems weak, despised, worthless.  Minorities, females, and the disabled all
become the abortionist’s prey in disproportionate numbers. Each individual
abortion may be an individual choice, but the aggregate paints a picture of
abortion as an instrument of oppression.

Margaret Sanger would be proud.
