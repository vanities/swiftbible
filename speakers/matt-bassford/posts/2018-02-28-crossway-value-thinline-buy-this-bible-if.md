Buy this Bible if:

* You’re looking for a relatively small, light, thin Bible.
* You want a translation that’s useful both for reading and
  close study.
* You prefer or at least tolerate double-column Bibles.
* You’re willing to use a paragraphed Bible.
* You want a Bible with at least light cross-referencing.
* You look for line-matched Bibles.
* You want a sewn Bible for as cheap as you can find one.



Don’t buy this Bible if:

* You want a Bible clad in the skin of a dead animal, and the
  mythical polybeast doesn’t count.
* You demand a single-column Bible.
* You can’t handle small print.
* You only like verse-by-verse Bibles.
* You prefer a full cross-reference suite.
* You want a Bible with good or even mediocre paper.
* You want to write in the margins of your Bible.
